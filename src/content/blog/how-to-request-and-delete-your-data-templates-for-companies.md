All right, guys, you have more power over your data than most companies want you to know. How to request deletion of personal data: send an access request to see what they hold, then a deletion request to make them erase it. In California, the CCPA gives residents the right to know what personal information a business collects, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing [1]. In Europe, the GDPR gives a "right of access" and a "right to erasure" [2]. And about two dozen US states now have comprehensive privacy laws with similar rights [3].

Here are access requests, deletion requests, templates and what to do if a company ignores you.

**Quick answer**

- Data subject access request: ask a company what data it has about you.
- Right to delete personal data: ask it to erase that data, where the law allows [1][2].
- Data deletion request template: short, specific, with your identifying details and a deadline.
- If ignored: complain to your state attorney general or privacy agency.

## Access requests

An access request asks: what do you have, where did you get it, and who did you share it with? Under the GDPR, companies generally must answer within one month [2]. Under the CCPA, businesses generally have 45 days [1]. This is how you find out [who is buying your personal information](/blog/data-brokers/).

## Deletion requests

A deletion request asks the company to erase your data. There are exceptions: companies can keep data needed for legal obligations, security or completing a transaction [1][2].

## Templates

**Access request:**

> Subject: Request to know / access my personal data
>
> I am requesting a copy of the personal information you hold about me, the sources you got it from, the purposes for using it, and the third parties you shared or sold it to. My details: [name], [email], [phone], [address]. Please respond within the time required by law.

**Deletion request:**

> Subject: Request to delete my personal data
>
> I request that you delete all personal information you hold about me and direct your service providers and any third parties you sold or shared it with to do the same. My details: [name], [email], [phone], [address]. Please confirm in writing when this is complete.

**Opt-out of sale/sharing:**

> Subject: Do not sell or share my personal information
>
> I direct you not to sell or share my personal information. My details: [name], [email].

## If a company ignores you

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **Keep copies** of every request and reply.
2. **Send one reminder** with the date of your first request.
3. **Complain** to your state attorney general or privacy agency; in California, the CPPA or the Attorney General [1].
4. **Know the rules** in your state: [what the law gives you](/blog/gdpr-us-state-privacy-laws/).
5. **Opt out of people-search sites** separately: [opt-out steps for people-search sites](/blog/remove-yourself-from-data-brokers/).

## FAQ

**What is a data subject access request?** A request to see what data a company holds about you [2].

**Do I have a right to delete personal data?** In California, the EU and many states, yes, with exceptions [1][2][3].

**Is there a data deletion request template?** Yes, use the ones above.

**How long do companies have to reply?** Generally one month under GDPR, 45 days under CCPA [1][2].

**What if they ignore me?** Complain to the regulator.

## What to do next

1. Send an access request to one company you use daily.
2. Save the reply.
3. Send a deletion request if you don't like what you see.
