All right, guys, I've seen it all over: "Trump went after Obama in the speech." Did he? Let's go to the transcript, because that's the only way to settle it. Yes, Trump mentioned Barack Obama in the July 16, 2026 address, but in one specific context: he referred to "burn bags" of documents, saying material was "given by President Barack Hussein Obama to be burned" [1]. TIME also noted the "burn bags" allegation and described it as unverified [2]. What the transcript does not contain: a statement that Obama rigged 2020, or a call to charge Obama with a crime. The investigative language in the speech is general: "investigate how and why such crucial information was hidden," and prosecute "those responsible for any crimes" [1].
