All right, guys, here's a deal that changed what Aladdin covers. On May 10, 2019, BlackRock completed its acquisition of eFront, a Paris-based software company founded in 1999 [1]. eFront called itself "the leading technology provider for managing the alternatives investment lifecycle" [1]. The point of the deal, in BlackRock's words: "vastly expanding Aladdin's alternatives capabilities and providing a whole-portfolio technology solution to clients" [1].

Then in 2025, BlackRock added Preqin, a private markets data provider, for about $3.2 billion [2]. Here's what eFront does, the acquisition, why private markets matter and how it connects to Aladdin.

**Quick answer**

- eFront acquisition: completed May 10, 2019 [1].
- What eFront does: software for private equity, real estate and other alternatives [1].
- Employees at the time: 700 [1].
- Aladdin private markets: eFront plus Preqin data, bought in 2025 [2].

## What does eFront do?

Its software covers the private markets lifecycle, "from due diligence and portfolio planning through performance and risk analysis," across several alternative asset classes [1]. Users include investors in private equity, real estate, banking and insurance [1]. See [the wider product line](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-products/).

## Why do private markets matter?

Because that's where money is moving: private equity, private credit, infrastructure. BlackRock bought HPS, a private credit firm, in July 2025, and GIP, an infrastructure firm, in October 2024 [2]. I need you to see the pattern: the software and the funds grew in the same direction.

## How does it connect to Aladdin?

eFront brings private holdings into the same view as public stocks and bonds, gaining Aladdin analytics while staying its own platform [1]. Preqin adds the market data [2].
