All right, guys, I've seen posts saying EO 13848 means "they" can take your house, your bank account and your passport if you helped steal the election. Let's check that against the text. The order blocks the property of foreign persons who are designated by Treasury for election interference [1]. Blocking is a freeze: the owner keeps title but can't use, sell or move the property without a license [2]. It's not confiscation. The order also suspends entry into the United States for blocked foreign persons [1]. That's an entry ban for foreigners, not the cancellation of an American's passport. Under IEEPA, outright confiscation is allowed only "when the United States is engaged in armed hostilities or has been attacked," and even then against foreign persons or countries that took part [3].

Here are the claims, what the text says, blocking vs taking, and entry limits vs passports.

**Quick answer**

- Can EO 13848 take your house: no; it blocks property of designated foreign persons [1].
- Blocking: a freeze, not a seizure; ownership stays put [2].
- Passports: the order restricts entry by foreigners; it doesn't revoke U.S. passports [1].
- Confiscation: IEEPA allows it only in armed hostilities or after an attack [3].

## What the text says

Section 2 blocks "all property and interests in property" of designated foreign persons that are in the U.S. or controlled by U.S. persons [1]. Section 6 suspends their entry [1].

## Blocking vs taking

- **Blocking**: frozen; owner keeps title; transactions need an OFAC license [2].
- **Confiscation under IEEPA**: only in armed hostilities or after an attack [3].
- **Civil or criminal forfeiture**: needs a separate law and a court process.

## Entry limits vs passports

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

A passport is issued to U.S. nationals by the State Department under passport law. Section 6 of the order is about foreigners' entry into the U.S. under immigration law section 212(f) [1]. It doesn't touch U.S. passports. If someone tells you it does, ask for the line. Treason, the other big claim, is a very specific crime: [a very specific crime](/blog/eo-13848-treason/).

## FAQ

**Can EO 13848 take my house?** No; it blocks property of designated foreign persons [1].

**What does blocking mean?** A freeze, not a transfer of ownership [2].

**Can it cancel my passport?** No; it restricts entry by blocked foreigners [1].

**Can IEEPA ever confiscate property?** Only in armed hostilities or after an attack [3].

**Who decides blocking?** Treasury, after consultation [1].
