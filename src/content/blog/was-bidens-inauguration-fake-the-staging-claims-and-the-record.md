All right, guys, let's take the big question head on: was Biden's inauguration fake? The claim says the January 20, 2021 ceremony was staged, on a set or a green screen. Patel Patriot gave it a name: Biden was "fauxnaugurated" [1]. The law sets the date: the president's term ends "at noon on the 20th day of January" [2].

Here are the staging claims, the "fake White House" stories, what evidence exists and why it matters for the larger story.

**Quick answer**

- Fake inauguration claim: the ceremony was a show [1].
- Biden inauguration green screen: a version that says the backdrop was digital.
- Record: Trump spoke at Joint Base Andrews that morning and wished "the new administration great luck" [3].
- Law: the 20th Amendment ends the term at noon on January 20 [2].

## What are the staging claims?

The [fake biden conspiracy theory](/blog/fake-biden-conspiracy-theory/) has several pieces about that day:

| Claim | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Biden inauguration staged | The ceremony was performed, not real [1] |
| Green screen | The Capitol backdrop was added digitally |
| Empty crowd | The small crowd proves it was fake |
| Odd details | Weather, shadows or times don't match |

The empty crowd point has a public explanation: the 2021 ceremony was held under COVID-19 rules and heavy security after January 6. That explains the look, but believers read it as part of the staging.

## Sets, signs and 'fake White House' stories

Patel Patriot wrote that Biden is "on a set replica of the WH almost always" [1]. That idea grew into a whole set of stories. See [stories about a fake White House and film sets](/blog/fake-white-house-set/).

## What evidence exists?

Here's what the record shows for January 20, 2021:

| Time | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | Trump's departure remarks at Joint Base Andrews | [3] |
| Noon | The president's term ends under the 20th Amendment | [2] |

Trump said that morning: "I wish the new administration great luck and great success" [3]. And: "We will be back in some form" [3]. Believers point to the second line. Both are on the official record.

I need you to see what a fake would need. A staged ceremony would need the press pool, the Capitol staff, the security services and the guests all in on it. That's thousands of people.

## Why does it matter for the larger story?

Because the inauguration is the hinge of the whole shadow presidency story. If it was real, the question becomes who governed after it. If it was fake, everything after is a show. Part 8 of devolution takes a third path: "Biden assuming office had to happen" [4].
