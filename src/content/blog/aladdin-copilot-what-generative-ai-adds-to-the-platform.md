All right, guys, AI is everywhere, and Aladdin has it too. Aladdin Copilot is a generative AI tool for clients of BlackRock's Aladdin platform. Microsoft's write-up says it "serves to strengthen the connections across the Aladdin platform," surfacing answers quickly for business decisions [1]. It also draws a clear line: "Aladdin Copilot will not give investment advice or respond to questions outside the Aladdin platform boundaries" [1].

So it's an assistant inside the software, not a brain running the markets. Here's what it is, the typical tasks, what it doesn't do and the questions of accuracy and control.

**Quick answer**

- BlackRock Aladdin Copilot: an AI assistant for Aladdin users [1].
- Aladdin generative AI: answers questions about the user's data on the platform [1].
- Limits: no investment advice, nothing outside the platform [1].
- Hosting: Aladdin runs on Microsoft Azure [1][2].

## What doesn't it do?

I need you to hear this clearly. Per the description, it:

1. **Doesn't give investment advice** [1].
2. **Doesn't answer questions outside the Aladdin platform** [1].
3. **Works within user permissions** [1].

It includes safeguards such as content filtering and limits on hallucination [1].

## What about accuracy and control?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Any AI can be wrong. The questions to ask:

1. **Where does the answer come from?** The user's own data on the platform.
2. **Who checks it?** The human using it.
3. **Who's responsible?** The firm, not the software.
