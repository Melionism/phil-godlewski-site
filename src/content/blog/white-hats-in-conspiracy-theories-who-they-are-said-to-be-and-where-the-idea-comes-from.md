All right, guys, let's talk about the good guys. In the white hats conspiracy theory, white hats are patriots inside the military and the government who work a hidden plan against a corrupt network. The image is old: in movie westerns, the law-abiding heroes wore white hats, and the phrase "white hat" for an honorable person dates to 1965 [1].

In the devolution series, the white hats military role goes to the Combatant Commanders under Chris Miller [2]. Here's where the idea came from, and how it explains 2020, the handover and the scandals.

**Quick answer**

- Who are the white hats: hidden good actors in the military and government [1][2].
- White hats in QAnon: Q used the phrase in post 3312 (2019) and post 4960 (2022) [3].
- White hats in devolution: the Combatant Commanders and Chris Miller [2].
- White hats meaning outside conspiracy talk: an honorable person, or a hacker who finds flaws so they can be fixed [1].

## Where does the white hat image come from?

From Hollywood. Merriam-Webster traces it to "the white hats stereotypically worn by law-abiding characters in movie westerns" [1]. The computer world picked it up later for hackers who test systems so they can be fixed [1].

The movement gave it a third meaning. On April 11, 2019, Q post 3312 used it about Robert Mueller and a "disinformation push" [3]. On November 7, 2022, post 4960 said: "WHITE HATs have secured many systems, but problems still remain" [3]. White hats are one of the pillars of the [trump shadow presidency theory](/blog/trump-shadow-presidency-theory/).

## How does the theory explain the handover of power?

Part 8, "How & When," published August 9, 2021, says: "Biden assuming office had to happen" [6]. It argues Vice President Pence's role on January 6 was limited to counting the votes as cast [6].

The rules for counting electoral votes on January 6 are in 3 U.S.C. § 15, and the presiding officer is the President of the Senate, that is, the Vice President [7]. Congress rewrote that section in December 2022, and the current text limits the presiding officer to ministerial duties [7]. ## Who are the white hats up against?

The other side is usually called the cabal. Here's something a lot of people don't know. A search of Q's archive finds "cabal" only in replies from anons, starting November 24, 2017, and never in Q's own text [13].

So the two words have different roots. "White hat" is an old English phrase that Q picked up [1][3]. "Cabal" came from the community around Q [13].

## What can actually be verified?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Some parts you can check today:

1. **EO 13848, signed September 12, 2018.** It requires a foreign-interference assessment within 45 days after an election [8].

3. **EO 13961, signed December 7, 2020.** It created the Executive Committee Part 13 calls the shadow government [4][11].
