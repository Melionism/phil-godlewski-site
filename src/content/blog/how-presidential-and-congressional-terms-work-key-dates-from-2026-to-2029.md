All right, guys, a lot of confusion after an election comes from not knowing the calendar. The Constitution fixes most of it. Under the 20th Amendment, the terms of senators and representatives end at noon on January 3, and the President's term ends at noon on January 20 [1]. House members serve two years; senators serve six, with a third of the Senate up every two years; the President serves four [1]. So the Congress elected on November 3, 2026, takes office on January 3, 2027, and serves until January 3, 2029 [1][2]. Trump's term runs until noon on January 20, 2029 [1]. The next federal election is November 7, 2028, the Tuesday after the first Monday in November, as federal law sets it [3]. In between, Congress counts the electoral votes on January 6, 2029 [3].

Here's Congress, the presidency, the calendar, and what falls where.

**Quick answer**

- Presidential and congressional term dates: Congress starts January 3; the President on January 20 [1].
- New Congress: January 3, 2027 [1][2].
- Next election: November 7, 2028 [3].
- Next inauguration: January 20, 2029 [1].
