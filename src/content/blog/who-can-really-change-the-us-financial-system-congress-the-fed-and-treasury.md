All right, guys, here's the question behind every "reset" video: who controls the US financial system? Nobody alone. Congress writes the laws. The Federal Reserve sets monetary policy under powers Congress gave it [1]. Treasury manages the government's money and debt. Bank regulators supervise banks. A real change needs a law, a rule or a policy decision with a public record. The GENIUS Act, signed July 18, 2025, is a good example: a bill, a vote, a signature, a public law number [2].

Here's Congress, the Federal Reserve, Treasury and regulators, and how a real change happens.

**Quick answer**

- Who controls the US financial system: shared between Congress, the Fed, Treasury and regulators.
- Who regulates US banks: the Fed, the FDIC and the OCC, depending on the bank.
- Congress, Fed, Treasury roles: laws, monetary policy, government finances.
- Who can change the financial system: Congress, by passing a law [2].

## Congress

Congress created the Fed and can change its powers [1]. It wrote the stablecoin law in 2025 [2]. If a change touches the money in your account, Congress is usually where it starts. Here's [the plumbing behind your bank account](/blog/financial-system-works/).

## The Federal Reserve

The Fed sets interest rates and supervises some banks [1]. It isn't "owned" by anyone, and its Reserve Banks send their net earnings to the Treasury [3].

## Treasury and regulators

| Body | Main job |
| --- | --- |
| Congress | Writes laws [2] |
| Federal Reserve | Monetary policy, some bank supervision [1] |
| Treasury | Government finances, debt, sanctions |
| FDIC | Deposit insurance, bank supervision |
| OCC | Supervises national banks |
| President | Signs or vetoes laws, issues executive orders [4] |

An executive order directs agencies; it doesn't replace a law. EO 14178, for example, barred federal agencies from creating a US central bank digital currency [4].

## How does a real change happen?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **A bill is introduced** and gets a number.
2. **Both chambers vote.**
3. **The President signs** it into law [2].
4. **Agencies write rules** with public comment.
5. **The rules take effect** on a set date.

Every step leaves a record. Algorithms are already part of this world too: [what algorithms already do at your bank](/blog/ai-finance/).

## FAQ

**Who controls the US financial system?** Congress, the Fed, Treasury and regulators share it [1][2].

**Who regulates US banks?** The Fed, FDIC and OCC, depending on the charter.

**What are the roles of Congress, the Fed and Treasury?** Laws, monetary policy, federal finances.

**Who can change the financial system?** Congress, by law; agencies, by rules.

**Is the Fed private?** The Fed says it isn't owned by anyone [3].
