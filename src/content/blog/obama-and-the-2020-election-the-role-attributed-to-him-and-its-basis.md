All right, guys, in parts of our community, there's a theory that Barack Obama was the real power behind the 2020 result, running things from the shadows. Let's look at it the way we'd look at any claim: what's alleged, what it rests on, and what's on the public record. What's on the record about Obama in 2020: he campaigned publicly for Joe Biden, his former vice president. What's on the record from his critics: in July 2025, DNI Tulsi Gabbard released documents arguing the Obama administration politicized the 2016 Russia assessment, referred them to the Justice Department, and Trump called it "treason" [1]. FactCheck.org found that case misleading, and it concerns 2016, not the 2020 count [1]. I haven't found a document, court finding or official statement showing Obama directed the 2020 vote count.

Here's what's claimed, the basis, the public record and the open questions.

**Quick answer**

- Obama role 2020 election claims: that he orchestrated or controlled the outcome.
- Obama 2020 election conspiracy: no document or ruling supports it publicly.
- What did Obama do in the 2020 election: campaigned for Biden.
- Obama shadow government claim: tied to disputes over 2016 intelligence, which are separate [1].

## Basis

The White House's July 2026 release quotes an FBI official saying, "I'm basically running a shadow government across the FBI at this point" [2]. Supporters see that as a smoking gun. Critics say it's an informal remark, without context, that doesn't mention Obama or vote counting.
