All right, guys, the fight over election rules ran for nearly two years, and it's easy to lose track. So here it is in order, with dates you can check. It started on March 25, 2025, when Trump signed Executive Order 14248 on proof of citizenship and mail ballot deadlines [1]. Courts blocked key parts that spring and summer, including Judge Denise Casper's ruling for 19 states on June 13, 2025 [1]. In 2026 the fight moved to Congress: the House passed the SAVE America Act 218-213 in February, and the Senate debated it in March without breaking the 60-vote filibuster [2][3]. Trump signed a second order, EO 14399, on March 31, 2026 [4]. Judge Indira Talwani blocked parts of it on June 25; the First Circuit refused to pause her ruling in July; the Supreme Court said in August that the challenge was premature, and in September refused to let a Postal Service mail ballot rule take effect [5][6]. Then the country voted on November 3, 2026 [7].

Here's 2025, early 2026, the run-up to the vote, and after.

**Quick answer**

- Election rules fight timeline 2025 2026: two executive orders, one big bill, many court rulings.
- 2025: EO 14248 signed March 25; key parts blocked [1].
- 2026: SAVE America Act passed House, stalled in Senate; EO 14399 signed March 31 [2][3][4].
- Courts: Talwani, First Circuit, Supreme Court rulings through September [5][6].
