All right, guys, there's one sentence in EO 13848 that both sides love to quote. The order's preamble, signed by President Trump in 2018, says: "Although there has been no evidence of a foreign power altering the outcome or vote tabulation in any United States election, foreign powers have historically sought to exploit America's free and open political system" [1]. That sentence was repeated, with "outcomes" in the plural, in every yearly renewal notice from 2019 through 2025, including Trump's in 2020, Biden's in 2021 and 2024, and Trump's again in August 2025 [2][3][4][5]. Then, in the renewal signed August 31, 2026, it's gone. The 2026 notice opens its second paragraph with "Foreign powers have historically sought to exploit America's free and open political system" and says nothing about evidence either way [6].

Here's the wording, where it appears, what it means and why it matters.

**Quick answer**

- No evidence altered results EO 13848: the 2018 preamble says there was no evidence a foreign power altered any U.S. election outcome or vote count [1].
- Repeated: in renewal notices through August 2025 [2][5].
- Dropped: from the August 31, 2026 notice [6].
- Meaning: a statement about evidence as of each signing date, not a permanent finding.

## What it means

It's a statement of what officials said they had at the time of signing. It isn't a court ruling. Two readings people make:

1. **"Even Trump admitted no foreign power changed votes."** Supporters of the official result point to it, including in the 2020 and 2025 notices.
2. **"It only covers foreign powers, and only what was known."** Skeptics note it says nothing about domestic issues and could change with new evidence.

Both are accurate descriptions of the text's scope. What changed in 2026 is that the sentence was removed. The notice doesn't explain why [6]. That's a fact you can check; why it was removed is something the document doesn't tell us.

## Why it matters

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

The order's own process produced findings on 2020: the DNI found "no indications that any foreign actor attempted to alter any technical aspect of the voting process," and DOJ and DHS found "no evidence that any foreign government-affiliated actor manipulated election results" [7][8]. ## FAQ

**What does the "no evidence" sentence say?** No foreign power was shown to have altered any U.S. election outcome or vote count [1].

**Did Biden's notices include it?** Yes, including 2021 and 2024 [3][4].

**Did Trump's 2025 notice include it?** Yes [5].

**Is it in the 2026 notice?** No [6].

**Does its removal prove anything?** The notice gives no reason, so it doesn't prove a new finding.
