All right, guys, Aladdin isn't one box. It's a family. The BlackRock Aladdin products today include Aladdin Enterprise, Aladdin Wealth, eFront, Aladdin Climate, Preqin data and Aladdin Copilot [1][2][3][4]. BlackRock's 2025 10-K calls Aladdin Enterprise "the Company's proprietary technology platform" and says eFront and Aladdin can work together in a "Whole Portfolio" solution [1].

Here's the Aladdin product suite, piece by piece, and how to think about choosing between competing suites.

**Quick answer**

- Aladdin Enterprise: the core platform [1].
- Aladdin Wealth: for wealth managers and advisors [1].
- eFront: private markets software, acquired May 2019 [2].
- Aladdin Climate: climate risk analytics, December 2020 [3].
- Aladdin Copilot: generative AI on the platform [4].

## FAQ

**What are BlackRock Aladdin products?** Enterprise, Wealth, eFront, Climate, Preqin data and Copilot [1][2][3][4].

**What is the Aladdin product suite?** The set of modules built on the Aladdin platform [1].
