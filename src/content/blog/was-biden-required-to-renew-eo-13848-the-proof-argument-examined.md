All right, guys, you've probably heard this one: "Biden had to renew 13848 because it proves foreign interference in 2020, and if he let it go, it would expose everything." Let's look at it honestly. Biden wasn't required to renew it. Under the National Emergencies Act, an emergency simply ends on its anniversary if the President doesn't publish a continuation notice, and a President can also end one early by proclamation [1]. Biden did end some Trump-era emergencies: the southern border emergency in January 2021 and the International Criminal Court sanctions emergency in April 2021 [2][3]. He chose to keep EO 13848, and he used it. His Treasury designated 29 people and entities under it on April 15, 2021, including Konstantin Kilimnik and Kremlin official Alexei Gromov, and more Iranian and Russian targets through 2024 [4][5].

Here's the answer, why he kept and used it, the argument and its flaw, and a reverse test.

**Quick answer**

- Was Biden required to renew EO 13848: no; renewal is discretionary [1].
- Proof he could have stopped: he ended other Trump emergencies [2][3].
- Why he kept it: it's a working sanctions tool he used against Russia and Iran [4][5].
- His notices also said there was "no evidence of a foreign power altering the outcomes or vote tabulation" [6].

## The argument and its flaw

**The argument:** renewal means 2020 interference was proven and covered up.

**The flaw:** the notices Biden signed said the opposite on vote-changing: "no evidence of a foreign power altering the outcomes or vote tabulation" [6]. Renewal shows the emergency continues, not what happened in any one election.

## A reverse test

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

Trump renewed EO 13848 in 2019, before the 2020 election, and again in 2025 and 2026 [8]. If renewal proved a stolen election, Trump's 2019 renewal would have "proved" something about 2018. It didn't. Other emergencies Biden kept in place show the same pattern: [other emergencies Biden kept in place](/blog/other-trump-orders-biden-extended/).
