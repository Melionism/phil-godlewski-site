All right, guys, I'm going to go through ten of the most repeated 2020 claims, one by one: what's claimed, what the record shows, and the conclusion. Some of you believe some of these. I'm not here to mock anybody. I'm here to put the claim next to the evidence so you can judge. The big picture: Georgia hand-counted every presidential ballot and upheld the result [1]; Michigan's hand count in Antrim County matched the machine totals [2]; election officials, including Trump's own cybersecurity agency, found no evidence any voting system "deleted or lost votes, changed votes, or was in any way compromised" [3]; and the publisher of 2000 Mules withdrew the film and apologized [4].

Here are votes and machines, ballots, courts, and how to check more.

**Quick answer**

- 2020 election fact check: the most popular claims didn't hold up in recounts, audits or courts [1][2][3].
- Common 2020 election claims: switched votes, dead voters, vote dumps, ballot mules, suitcases of ballots.
- 2020 election debunked: hand counts, audits and court rulings tested the main claims.
- How to check: go to the original record, not the clip.

## Votes and machines

| # | Claim | Evidence | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dominion machines switched votes | Georgia hand count matched; Antrim hand count matched [1][2] | Not supported |
| 2 | Antrim County proved machine fraud | Human error in an unofficial report, caught and fixed; hand count matched [2] | Error, not fraud |
| 3 | A foreign server or satellite flipped votes | Officials found no system compromised [3] | Not supported |
| 4 | "Vote dumps" at night prove fraud | Big batches of mail ballots reported at once | Normal reporting |

## How to check more

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **Find the original document**, not a summary.
2. **Ask: error or fraud?** See [three words often mixed up](/blog/election-error-vs-fraud/).
3. **Ask: how many votes?** See [the arithmetic of the outcome](/blog/many-votes-change-2020-election/).
4. **Look for the official answer** from the state.

## FAQ

**Did Dominion switch votes?** Hand counts didn't support it [1][2].

**Did dead people vote?** A few isolated cases, not enough to matter.

**What was wrong in Antrim County?** A human error in unofficial results [2].

**Were there ballot mules?** The film's publisher retracted the claims [4].

**Did courts look at evidence?** Some did, and rejected it [5].
