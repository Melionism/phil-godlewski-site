All right, guys, devolution theory explained, plain and simple. Devolution theory says Trump used a real continuity-of-government mechanism before January 20, 2021 to keep authority with loyal officials and the military [1][2]. The term itself is official: FEMA defines devolution as moving an agency's authority to backup staff and facilities in a catastrophic emergency [3].

So there are two meanings here. One is the government term. The other is the theory built on top of it. I need you to keep them apart while you read.

**Quick answer**

- What is devolution in government: a continuity plan to transfer authority when the main staff cannot work [3].
- What is the devolution theory: the claim that Trump used such a plan in 2020–2021 [2].
- Who created it: Patel Patriot, starting July 2, 2021 [1].
- Size: 25 numbered parts and 5 addenda [4].

## What does devolution mean in real government planning?

Continuity of government devolution is written into federal rules. FEMA's Federal Continuity Directive 1, dated January 17, 2017, sets it as a standing capability for every agency [3]. A devolution plan is activated "upon the threat of, or in response to a catastrophic emergency" [3].

The public got a look at it on March 18, 2020. William Arkin reported in Newsweek on a "line of devolution," a duplicate chain of officials outside Washington, and on plans codenamed Octagon, Freejack and Zodiac [5]. That article is where Part 1 of the series starts [1]. For the bigger picture, see [a breakdown of the Trump shadow president claim](/blog/trump-shadow-presidency-theory/).

| Term | Official meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Devolution | Transfer of authority to backup staff and facilities | [3] |
| Continuity of government | Keeping essential functions running in an emergency | [3][6] |
| Line of devolution | A backup chain of officials outside Washington | [5] |

## Who created the theory?

Patel Patriot, the pen name of Jon Herold. He published Part 1 on Substack on July 2, 2021, after Trump's farewell speech left him asking questions: "None of this made sense" [1]. Get the full story of the [patel patriot devolution origin](/blog/patel-patriot-devolution-origin/) on its own page.

He calls it a theory in his own words: "it's still just a theory… for now" [2]. Part 13, published November 4, 2021, goes further and says it is "no longer a question of if devolution is real" [7].

The series stands on public paper plus a few classified items. Here are the main ones and how the author reads each.

I need you to see this part clearly. EO 13961 speaks of "continuity of operations, continuity of government, and enduring constitutional government" [6]. The word "devolution" is not in it. The link is the author's reading [2][6].

## Does devolution require Trump's return?

Patel Patriot answers that one in a post of its own. He leans on the continuity phrase "resume normal operations" and writes: "The 'normal operations' before devolution was initiated had Trump as the President of the United States" [10].

He adds two limits. "Any Trump 'return' will have to be constitutionally sanctioned" [10]. And: "Whatever your theory is of any devolvement, we need to understand it only applies to the Executive branch" [10].

> **In short:** in the author's view, devolution ends with Trump back in office, by a constitutional route, and it covers the executive branch only [10].

Devolution grew in the same community, but it works differently. Q posted anonymously in short drops; Patel Patriot signs his name and writes long parts with footnotes [2][11].
