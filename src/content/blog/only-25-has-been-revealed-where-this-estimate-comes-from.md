All right, guys, after the July 16 address, a line started going around: "Only 25% has been revealed. The rest is coming." I've heard it on streams and seen it in comments. So let's do what we always do and trace it. I went to the source material: the full transcript of the speech doesn't contain that figure; the only percentages Trump used that night were about drug prices and business expensing [1]. The White House's own release on the documents doesn't say how much more will be published [2]. I couldn't find an official statement giving that number. That doesn't mean nothing else is coming. It means "25%" is a number without a documented source, and you should treat it that way.

Here's the claim, what can be checked, classification, secrecy logic, past promises, how to read the next release, and what proof would need.

**Quick answer**

- Only 25 percent election evidence revealed: not in the transcript or the White House release [1][2].
- 25 percent claim election documents: circulates in online commentary without an official source.
- Hidden election evidence claim: possible in principle, but unverifiable until released.
- More election documents to come: the White House hasn't given a scope [2].
