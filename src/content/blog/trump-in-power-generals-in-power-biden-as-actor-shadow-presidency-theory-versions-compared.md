## Was Trump secretly in charge?

This is the "trump still in power theory," and it's the core of [devolution theory](/blog/devolution-theory/). Part 4, "A Wartime President," says it in one line: "I don't think he ever 'left'" [1]. The same part argues Trump may have used a Presidential Emergency Action Document to suspend the counting of electoral votes [1].

Part 13 adds the people. It says the Executive Committee created by Executive Order 13961 worked as the shadow government, with Christopher Miller, John Ratcliffe, Robert O'Brien and others, and Mark Meadows as a "back channel" [5].

I said it myself on my July 19, 2026 show: "you know who was really running the country? The shadow regime, and that was the Trump regime" [6]. I pointed to Pompeo, Ratcliffe and the old Trump cabinet still meeting foreign leaders after they left office [6].

## Was the military running the country?

The "military running country theory" puts the generals in the driver's seat. Part 9, "The Military," says the Combatant Commanders "play an integral role in Devolution" [2]. It also gives the structure: "Chris Miller would be the CEO and the Combatant Commanders would be the heads of their respective departments" [2].

Part 9 makes a point about the chain of command too: "The Joint Chiefs of Staff are nowhere within the Chain of Command" [2]. Part 8, published August 9, 2021, names December 15, 2020 as the date the military moved from planning to carrying out devolution [7].



## Was Biden only an actor?

The "biden actor theory" is the most visual one. In "Does Devolution Require Trump's Return?", Patel Patriot calls the inauguration "fauxnaugurated" and says Biden is "on a set replica of the WH almost always" [3]. He also uses the name "byeden administration" [3].

On my July 19, 2026 show I called it "a massive central casting. It's a movie" [6]. Here's why that's important to me. I asked why Biden kept renewing Executive Order 13848 if he were "the real Joe Biden" [6]. He did continue its national emergency on September 7, 2021 [8] and again on September 9, 2024 [9].

## What do all three versions agree on?

More than you'd think. All three start from the same place and point to the same end. Here's the common ground:

- **The 2020 result was not legitimate.** Part 4 calls Biden's presidency illegitimate [1].
- **A continuity plan was in motion.** Part 13 ties it to Executive Order 13961, signed December 7, 2020 [5].
- **Trump comes back.** "Any Trump 'return' will have to be constitutionally sanctioned" [3].
- **It covers the executive branch.** "It only applies to the Executive branch" [3].
