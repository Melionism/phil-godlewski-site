All right, guys, let's talk numbers, because this isn't a small group. In June 2026, an Economist/YouGov poll found 28% of all Americans said the 2020 presidential election was "rigged," including 50% of Republicans, 66% of Republicans who identify as MAGA, and 9% of Democrats [1]. Back in December 2020, an NPR/PBS NewsHour/Marist poll found only 24% of Republicans accepted the outcome, while 61% of Americans overall trusted the results [2]. So doubt has softened somewhat among Republicans over six years, but it's still massive.

Here are the poll results, who doubts, why, and how views changed.

**Quick answer**

- How many Americans think 2020 was stolen: about 28% said "rigged" in June 2026 [1].
- 2020 election polls Republicans: 50% said rigged in 2026; only 24% accepted the outcome in December 2020 [1][2].
- Trust in 2020 results: 61% of Americans overall in December 2020 [2].
- Trend: doubt among Republicans has declined but remains high.

## Why

I get it, I get it. People don't doubt for no reason. Here's what drives it:

1. **Trust in leaders**: when the leader you voted for says it was stolen, many believe him.
2. **The count itself**: early leads that vanished overnight looked suspicious.
3. **Rule changes** during the pandemic.
4. **Distrust of media and agencies**, sometimes earned in other cases.
5. **Repetition**: claims repeated for years feel true.

None of that is proof either way. It explains belief, not what happened. For proof, see the evidence guide in this series.

## How views changed

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." In 2020 three in four Republicans doubted the result [2]; by 2026 it was about half [1]. Views moved as audits, recounts and court cases came in, and as time passed. They also move with events, like the July 2026 address.
