All right, guys, Paul Furber's name comes up every time people talk about who started Q. Here's what's actually established and what isn't. Furber is a Johannesburg tech journalist who wrote for years for the South African site ITWeb and its Brainstorm magazine [1]. In the early days of Q, he was among the first promoters, and he ran a board where Q posted; he seemed to have a trusted relationship with Q [1]. In February 2022, two separate linguistic teams reported that Furber's writing style dominated the earliest Q posts, before Ron Watkins' style took over around the start of 2018 [2]. The New York Times summed it up as: "Paul Furber was its first apostle" [1]. Fredrick Brennan, who founded 8chan, said in 2020 that Furber was most likely the original Q [1]. Furber denies writing as Q, and argued instead that Q's posts influenced his own writing style [2]. No court or official body has made a finding on this, and Furber hasn't been charged with anything connected to Q.

Here's who he is, what's alleged, his response, and what's unproven.
