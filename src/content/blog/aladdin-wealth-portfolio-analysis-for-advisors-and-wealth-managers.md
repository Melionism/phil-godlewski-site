All right, guys, this is the piece of Aladdin closest to regular investors. Aladdin Wealth gives wealth management firms and their financial professionals "portfolio construction, modeling, and risk analytics tools," BlackRock's 2025 10-K says [1]. In plain words: it's the version of Aladdin built for advisors who manage money for families and individuals.

You won't log into it yourself. Your advisor might. Here's who it's for, what it analyzes, how advisors use it and what you should know as an individual.

**Quick answer**

- BlackRock Aladdin Wealth: Aladdin's tools for wealth firms and advisors [1].
- Aladdin Wealth for advisors: portfolio construction, models, risk analytics [1].
- Aladdin Wealth portfolio analysis: risk and exposure across a client's holdings.
- For you: ask your advisor what software they use and what it shows.

## Who is it for?

Banks, brokerages and wealth firms, and the advisors who work there [1]. It's part of [the full Aladdin product suite](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-products/).

| User | What they use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Wealth firm | Firm-wide model portfolios and risk view |
| Advisor | Analyzing a client's holdings, building proposals |
| Client | Sees reports the advisor shares |

## What does it analyze?

The same kinds of things Aladdin measures for institutions, scaled to a client account: how concentrated the holdings are, how they'd do in a stress scenario, and how they compare to a model portfolio [1].

## How do advisors use it?

1. **Load the client's holdings.**
2. **Run risk and exposure reports.**
3. **Compare with a model portfolio** the firm uses.
4. **Propose changes** to the client.

I need you to see step 4. The advisor proposes; you decide.

## What should individual investors know?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Ask your advisor:

1. Which platform do you use for my account?
2. What risk numbers can you show me?
3. Whose model portfolio are you comparing me to?
4. Are the funds you recommend from the same company as the software?

That last one matters with BlackRock, which sells both funds and software. See [how Aladdin scores climate risk](/blog/aladdin-climate/) and [generative AI inside Aladdin](/blog/aladdin-copilot/).

## FAQ

**What is Aladdin Wealth?** Aladdin's tools for wealth managers and advisors [1].

**What does BlackRock Aladdin Wealth do?** Portfolio construction, modeling and risk analytics [1].

**Is Aladdin Wealth for advisors only?** It's sold to firms; clients see the results through their advisor [1].

**What is Aladdin Wealth portfolio analysis?** Measuring risk and exposure in a client's holdings [1].

**Can I use Aladdin Wealth myself?** Not directly; ask your advisor about the tools they use.
