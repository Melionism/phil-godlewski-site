All right, guys, straight answer first. Did Pompeo visit China after the 2020 election? The public record shows no such trip. His last foreign tour as Secretary of State began in Paris on November 14, 2020 and covered seven countries, none of them China [1]. His planned January 2021 trip to Europe was cancelled on January 12, 2021 [2].

Then on January 20, 2021, China itself sanctioned him and barred him from entering the mainland, Hong Kong and Macao [3]. Here are the details, with the dates.

**Quick answer**

- Pompeo November 2020 trip: France, Turkey, Georgia, Israel, UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia [1].
- Pompeo cancelled Europe trip, January 2021: Brussels, announced and called off on January 12 [2].
- January 20, 2021: China sanctions Pompeo and 27 others [3].
- After leaving office, he visited Taiwan, March 2–5, 2022 [4].

## What is the claim?

The story says the outgoing Secretary of State went to China between the election and the inauguration, as part of a hidden team. It's one of the claims behind the [trump shadow cabinet](/blog/trump-shadow-cabinet-theory/) idea. I need you to check this one against the itinerary, because dates settle it.

## What was the November 2020 itinerary?

The Pompeo last foreign trip as Secretary started on Saturday, November 14, 2020, in Paris [1]. The countries, in order:

1. France
2. Turkey
3. Georgia
4. Israel
5. United Arab Emirates
6. Qatar
7. Saudi Arabia

China did come up, but as a topic. French Foreign Minister Jean-Yves Le Drian listed it among the "difficult subjects" for the talks, alongside Iraq, Iran and the Middle East [1]. Pompeo told the press: "I'm the secretary of state. I'm getting calls from all across the world" [1].

## What happened to the January 2021 trip?

It never happened. On January 12, 2021, less than 18 hours after the trip was announced, the State Department called it off [2]. The final stop was to be Brussels, to meet NATO's Jens Stoltenberg and Belgium's foreign minister. Luxembourg had already been dropped [2].

The reason given was the transition. Spokeswoman Morgan Ortagus said: "The Department of State is well along in its transition efforts with President-elect Biden's team" [2]. And: "we are cancelling all planned travel this week, including the secretary's trip to Europe" [2].

## What do official records show?

Here's the timeline in one table.

| Date | Event | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 14, 2020 | Seven-nation tour begins in Paris | [1] |
| Jan 12, 2021 | Europe trip announced, then cancelled | [2] |
| Jan 20, 2021 | China's Foreign Ministry sanctions 28 people, including Pompeo | [3] |
| Mar 2–5, 2022 | Pompeo visits Taiwan as a former official | [4] |

Here's why the January 20 date matters. China's statement bars the sanctioned people and their families from "entering the mainland, Hong Kong and Macao of China" [3]. When Pompeo went to Taiwan in 2022, Beijing's spokesman said: "Pompeo has long been sanctioned by China due to his anti-China actions and deeds" [4].
