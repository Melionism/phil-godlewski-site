## What do the documents show?

Here's why that's important. Do you remember what Trump signed in September 2018? I'll give you a hint: starts with an E and an O. Executive Order 13848, signed September 12, 2018 [3].

It sets a clock. Within 45 days after a US election, the Director of National Intelligence must assess any foreign interference [3]. For the November 3, 2020 election, that window closed on December 18, 2020. Then the Attorney General and Homeland Security have 45 days from that assessment to report [3].

On my show I asked why Biden kept that order alive [1]. He did: on September 7, 2021 he continued its national emergency for one year [8]. On September 9, 2024 he continued it again [9].
