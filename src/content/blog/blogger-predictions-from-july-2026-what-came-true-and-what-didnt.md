All right, guys, I've always said: hold people accountable for what they predict, and that includes me. Over the summer of 2026, a lot of predictions went around our community about the midterms. Now the vote is in, so let's score them honestly against the record. Some came true. The midterms were held on schedule on November 3, and Votebeat called it "generally a smooth day of voting" [1]. Some failed. The SAVE America Act didn't become law before the election; the Senate's last pre-election try, a standalone photo-ID bill from Sen. Jon Husted, failed a procedural vote 52-46 on August 8 [2]. There was no national voter ID by executive order, and the March 2026 order's mail ballot rule was blocked through Election Day [3]. Republicans didn't hold Congress; AP reported Democrats won both chambers [4]. And some can't be checked yet, like predictions about arrests in 2027.

Here's the full list, what came true, what failed, what can't be checked yet, and how to judge the next one.
