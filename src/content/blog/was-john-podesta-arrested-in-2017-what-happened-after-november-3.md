All right, guys, here's a straight answer to a question people still type into Google: no, John Podesta was not arrested in 2017. Here's the record. Q's drop #15 on October 31, 2017, said "11.3 - Podesta indicted," and drop #34 the next night said events would follow "the arrest announcement of Mr. Podesta (actionable 11.4)" [1][2]. November 3 and 4 came and went without any arrest or indictment of John Podesta, Hillary Clinton's 2016 campaign chairman. What did happen that week involved his brother. On October 30, 2017, the same day Special Counsel Robert Mueller's team unsealed charges against Paul Manafort, Tony Podesta stepped down from the Podesta Group lobbying firm, which had worked on a Ukraine-related campaign led by Manafort [3]. Tony said he "needs to fight this as an individual" [3]. Neither brother was charged. John Podesta went on to public roles: in January 2024 he was named senior adviser to the president for international climate policy, succeeding John Kerry [4]. Q's own later drops on November 9 and 20, 2017, said "DISINFORMATION IS NECESSARY" [5].

Here's the claim, what happened, the riots claim, and why it mattered.

**Quick answer**

- Was John Podesta arrested: no, not in 2017 or since.
- Q's dates: "11.3 - Podesta indicted"; arrest announcement 11.4 [1][2].
- That week: brother Tony Podesta left his firm amid the Mueller probe [3].
- Later: John Podesta named climate adviser in 2024 [4].
