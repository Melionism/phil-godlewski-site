All right, guys, here's the headline you've seen a hundred times: Aladdin "controls" tens of trillions. The real wording from BlackRock: "The platform is used to manage $25 trillion in assets globally" [1]. Assets on Aladdin platform means the total value of portfolios that firms run using the software. Each firm still owns or manages its own money and makes its own decisions.

Here's what's counted, who decides on the money, why the figure isn't control and how to read it.

**Quick answer**

- Aladdin assets on platform meaning: portfolios processed on the software [1].
- Aladdin platform assets: $25 trillion, per BlackRock [1].
- Aladdin assets controlled? No; clients' people decide [2].
- BlackRock's own AUM: $15.5 trillion, part of that total [3].

## What is being counted?

Every portfolio a client runs on Aladdin, added up, plus BlackRock's own [1][3]. It's a measure of how widely the software is used. See [why the total keeps rising](/blog/blackrock-assets-under-management/).

| Count | Meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $25 trillion | Assets managed on Aladdin by all users | [1] |
| $15.5 trillion | Assets BlackRock itself manages | [3] |
| Difference | Mostly other firms' money on the software | [1][3] |

## Who decides on the money?

The client firms. BlackRock's 10-K describes Aladdin as a platform for "risk analytics, portfolio management, trade execution, and investment operations" used by BlackRock and other investors [2]. The managers at each firm decide what to buy and sell.

## Why isn't the figure control?

I need you to see three points:

1. **Software doesn't own assets.** Clients do.
2. **Each firm sets its own rules** and strategies on the platform.
3. **Firms can leave.** BlackRock's 10-K warns clients may move to other tools [2].

That said, wide use of one system does raise fair questions about shared risk. That's the criticism side of the story.

## How do you read it?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." When you see a platform number:

1. **Check the source and date** [1].
2. **Check the verb:** "managed on," "used to manage," or "controls"?
3. **Don't add it to AUM.**
