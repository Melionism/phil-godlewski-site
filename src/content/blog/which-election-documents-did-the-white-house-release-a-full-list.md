All right, guys, everybody's talking about "the documents," but most people haven't seen the list. Here it is. Alongside the July 16, 2026 address, the White House posted declassified material on its website in four groups: election infrastructure, Chinese activity, a Michigan voter registration case, and noncitizens on voter rolls [1][2]. According to a detailed review by Lawfare, the items include a January 2020 National Intelligence Council memo, a partly declassified July 2020 CIA World Intelligence Review, a CISA report dated July 13, 2026 on election software assessments from 2019 to 2024, a June 29, 2026 CIA memo on Venezuela, a CIA summary of 2018–2020 reporting on China, a DHS one-pager on noncitizen registrations, and FBI files from the Michigan case [2].

Here's the list, what they say, what was already known and how to read the redactions.

**Quick answer**

- White House election documents released: four sets on infrastructure, China, Michigan and noncitizens [1][2].
- July 2026 election documents: posted on the White House transparency page [3].
- Declassified election documents: many pages are heavily redacted [4].
- Bottom line from reviewers: they document vulnerabilities and foreign activity, not changed votes [2][4].
