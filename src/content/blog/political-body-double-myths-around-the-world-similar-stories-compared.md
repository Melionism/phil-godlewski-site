All right, guys, the Biden double story isn't the first of its kind. Political body double myths go back a long way and show up in many countries. The best-known modern case is Vladimir Putin: on February 27, 2020, he told TASS he had been offered body doubles in the early 2000s and turned them down [1].

Stories about world leaders' body doubles follow a pattern. Once you see the pattern, you can test any new claim faster.

**Quick answer**

- Putin body double myth: Putin says he was offered doubles and refused [1].
- Biden: the May 2025 clone post and the "set replica" claim [2].
- Common thread: a leader looks or sounds different, and a double explains it.
- How to test: original photos, dates, independent sources.

## A long history of doubles

Leaders have used stand-ins for safety for a long time; that part is plain security practice. Putin's own words show the idea was on the table in Russia: "I declined these body doubles. This (the offer) was during the most difficult periods of the fight against terrorism" [1].

The myths start when people claim the double replaced the leader for good. That's the step the Biden story takes. See [viral Biden double photos checked](/blog/biden-clone-theory/).

## What do they have in common?

Four things show up every time:

1. **A change in looks.** Weight, face, hair, posture.
2. **A long absence.** Illness or time out of public view.
3. **Distrust of the official story.** The double explains what people already doubt.
4. **Photo comparisons.** Side-by-side images do the arguing.

With Biden, the third point came with official backing years later. The June 4, 2025 memorandum said "For years, President Biden suffered from serious cognitive decline" [3]. That fed the doubt, though the memo never mentions a double.

## How do you test such claims?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **Find the original image.** Not a repost or a screenshot.
2. **Check the date and place.** Lighting and lens change faces.
3. **Look for an independent record.** A schedule, a transcript, a statement.
4. **Ask what else would have to be true.** Who would know about the double?
