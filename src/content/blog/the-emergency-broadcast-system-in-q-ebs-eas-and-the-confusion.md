All right, guys, "EBS" is one of the most repeated phrases in Q circles: the idea that one day every TV and phone will light up with a message revealing everything. Drop 34 said the "Emergency Broadcast System (EMS)" would be used to message citizens [1]. Here's the catch: the Emergency Broadcast System doesn't exist anymore. It ran from the 1960s and was replaced by the Emergency Alert System in 1997 [2]. Today there are two main pieces. EAS interrupts radio and TV broadcasts with an alert. Wireless Emergency Alerts, launched in 2012, send short messages to phones, and presidential alerts can't be turned off [2]. These systems get tested publicly. There have been national EAS tests in November 2011, September 2016, September 2017, October 2018, August 2019, August 2021 and October 4, 2023, and a 2016 law requires a nationwide test at least every three years [2]. Every one of those tests came with an announced date and a message saying it was a test. In Q circles, each test or teaser gets read as a dress rehearsal. That's an interpretation. The documented record is routine testing.

Here are EBS and EAS, the myth, real tests, and how to read it.

**Quick answer**

- Emergency broadcast system QAnon: drop 34 promised EBS messages [1].
- Reality: EBS was replaced by EAS in 1997 [2].
- Phones: Wireless Emergency Alerts since 2012 [2].
- Tests: national tests, including October 4, 2023, announced in advance [2].
