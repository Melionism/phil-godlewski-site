All right, guys, a lot of people missed this, but the July 16 address ended with a specific ask. In the last two minutes of the 27-minute speech, President Trump urged Congress to pass the SAVE America Act and asked Americans to call their representatives [1][2]. The White House release also told Congress to "immediately pass the SAVE America Act" [3]. The bill would require proof of citizenship to register to vote, stricter voter ID, and sharing of voter rolls [2]. FactCheck.org noted that an amendment would also end most mail voting except for listed exceptions, and that its ID rules are stricter than those in most of the 36 states that already require voter ID [4].

Here's what he said, why now, the reaction and how it links to the bill.

**Quick answer**

- Trump SAVE Act speech: urged Americans to call Congress [2].
- SAVE Act election address: tied noncitizen registration figures to the bill [3].
- Status: needs 60 Senate votes; Republicans hold 53 [1].

## What he said

He said Congress must pass the SAVE America Act and called mail-in ballots "inherently corrupt" [5]. Allies quickly echoed it; see [what allies took from it](/blog/trump-election-speech-supporters-reaction/).

## Why now

1. **The midterms**: the speech came months before the November 2026 elections.
2. **The noncitizen figure**: DHS's ~278,000 claim was the setup [3].
3. **Pressure on the Senate**: the bill had stalled [1].
