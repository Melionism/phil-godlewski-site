All right, guys, let's take on the big one: why didn't the white hats stop Biden? The devolution series gives a direct answer. Part 8, published August 9, 2021, says: "Biden assuming office had to happen" [1]. In that reading, the white hats transfer of power went ahead on purpose, as one step in a longer plan [1][2].

Below: what believers claim, what the law required in January 2021, who took part, and what it means for the theory.

**Quick answer**

- The claim: the handover was allowed as part of the plan, not stopped [1].
- January 6: Congress counts electoral votes under 3 U.S.C. § 15 [3].
- January 20: the president's term ends at noon under the 20th Amendment [4].
- Trump that morning: "I wish the new administration great luck and great success" [5].

## What do believers claim?

There are three answers, and they come from different parts of the series. If you need the background first, read our page on the [white hats conspiracy theory](/blog/white-hats-conspiracy-theory/).

1. **It had to happen.** Part 8: "Biden assuming office had to happen" [1].
2. **Trump never really left.** Part 4, July 18, 2021: "I don't think he ever 'left'" [2].
3. **The inauguration was staged.** Patel Patriot calls it "fauxnaugurated" [6].

The "why trump left office theory" ties these together. Trump left the White House, but according to the series, authority stayed with the people around him [2][7].

## What did the 2021 procedure require?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Here is the procedure as written in law.

| Step | Date | What the law says | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Joint session of Congress | Jan 6, 2021 | Congress meets on January 6 to count the electoral votes | [3] |
| Presiding officer | Jan 6, 2021 | The President of the Senate, that is, the Vice President, presides | [3] |
| End of term | Jan 20, 2021 | "The terms of the President and Vice President shall end at noon on the 20th day of January" | [4] |

I need you to understand the presiding officer point, because the series builds on it. Part 8 argues Vice President Pence's role was limited to counting the votes as cast, and that he "gave Congress the opportunity to choose their fate" [1]. Congress rewrote 3 U.S.C. § 15 in December 2022, and the current text calls that role ministerial [3].

Part 4 offers a different path. It suggests Trump may have used a Presidential Emergency Action Document to suspend the electoral count [2]. PEADs are classified. In Part 25, the author himself warns: "Anybody claiming to know for a fact that a PEAD(s) has been implemented is lying" [8].

## What would a return have required?

Patel Patriot set the conditions himself. "Any Trump 'return' will have to be constitutionally sanctioned" [6]. He also says devolution "only applies to the Executive branch" [6].

So the handover in his view was never the end. It was a middle step, with the return as the last one. On January 20, 2025, Trump was back at the Capitol for his second inaugural address [12].
