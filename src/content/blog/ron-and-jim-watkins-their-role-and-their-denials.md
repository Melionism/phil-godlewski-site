All right, guys, if Q had a home, the Watkins family owned the house. Here's their role, what's been claimed, and what they say. Jim Watkins owns and runs 8chan, later renamed 8kun, the site where Q posted from early 2018 on [1][2]. His son Ron Watkins became 8chan's administrator in 2018 and resigned in November 2020 [3]. In 2022, two linguistic teams reported that Ron Watkins' writing style took over Q's posts around the start of 2018 [2]. In HBO's 2021 series "Q: Into the Storm," director Cullen Hoback concluded: "So yes, I do think Ron is Q." In one scene Ron said his research was "never as Q," then added: "Never as Q, I promise. Because I am not Q and I never was" [1]. Ron told CBS in October 2021: "I am not Q. I never have been Q" [3]. Hoback acknowledged nobody confessed, so his conclusion can't be confirmed [1]. In 2022 Ron ran for Congress in Arizona's 2nd District and got 3.5% in the Republican primary [3].

Here's their role, 8kun, denials, and what's unproven.
