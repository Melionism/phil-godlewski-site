All right, guys, "it's got the tripcode" is the line people use to say a post is really from Q. So let's understand what a tripcode actually is. On imageboards like 4chan and 8chan, there are no accounts. If you want readers to know two posts came from the same person, you type a password, and the site turns it into a short code shown next to your name. That code is a one-way hash of the password [1][2]. Anyone who knows the password can produce the same code. "Secure" tripcodes add a secret salt known only to the site's staff, so the same password gives a different code on each site [2]. That's why, when 8chan went down in 2019, Q couldn't prove identity on another website [2]. Q used several tripcodes over the years. A research team found that in March 2018 many of them were cracked, and in August 2018 the passwords were published on an online forum [1]. The same study found 299 tripcode posts that no aggregator archived [1]. So a tripcode shows continuity of a password. It doesn't show who's typing.

Here's how they work, how Q used them, limits, and compromise.

**Quick answer**

- What is a tripcode: a hash of a password shown next to a post [1][2].
- Proves: the poster knew the password.
- Doesn't prove: who the poster is.
- Q's codes: cracked in March 2018; passwords published in August 2018 [1].

## How they work

| Type | How | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Hash of password [2] | Can be cracked with time [2] |
| Secure | Hash plus site's secret salt [2] | Only works on one site [2] |

## Limits

- A password can be shared.
- A password can be stolen or cracked [1][2].
- A site's staff control secure-tripcode salts [2].

## Compromise

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- After that, anyone with the password could sign as Q.

## FAQ

**What is a tripcode?** A code made from a password to sign anonymous posts [1][2].

**Does a tripcode prove identity?** No; it proves knowledge of a password.

**Were Q's tripcodes compromised?** Yes, in 2018, per researchers [1].

**What's a secure tripcode?** One salted by the site [2].

**Could Q verify on another site?** Not with a secure tripcode [2].
