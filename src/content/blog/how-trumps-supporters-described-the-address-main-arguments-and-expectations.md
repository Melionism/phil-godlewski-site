All right, guys, let's start with how our side heard the July 16 address, in their own words. The White House published a long list of reactions [1]. Vice President JD Vance said: "Election integrity is not a partisan issue, it is an AMERICAN ISSUE." Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said: "Election security is national security." Speaker Mike Johnson said: "Only Americans should be deciding American elections." Sen. Cynthia Lummis said officials may have kept the president and Congress uninformed and that "Congress deserves a full accounting." Rep. Claudia Tenney called it a "BOMBSHELL." Former chief of staff Mark Meadows wrote: "278,000 reasons we need to pass the SAVE America Act." Former Pentagon official Ezra Cohen said: "The day has finally come" [1].

Here are supporters' arguments, critics and fact-checkers, John Solomon's statements, the SAVE America Act and bloggers' retellings.

**Quick answer**

- Trump election speech supporters reaction: praise for exposing foreign threats and hidden intelligence [1].
- Republican reaction election speech: calls to pass the SAVE America Act and for a "full accounting" [1].
- Conservative reaction Trump speech: commentators said China's data collection should be taken seriously [1].
- Expectations: more releases, investigations and the SAVE America Act.
