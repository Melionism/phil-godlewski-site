All right, guys, let's get the number right. How much money does BlackRock manage? $15.5 trillion at September 30, 2026, per its earnings release [1]. At the end of 2025 it was $14.0 trillion, with a 10% five-year growth rate [2]. At the end of 2024, $11.6 trillion [3].

Then there's a second number: Aladdin "is used to manage $25 trillion in assets globally," BlackRock says [4]. Those are two different things. Here's the BlackRock AUM growth, what each number means, how to verify it and why figures conflict online.

**Quick answer**

- BlackRock AUM: $15.5 trillion at September 30, 2026 [1].
- End of 2025: $14.0 trillion [2].
- End of 2024: $11.6 trillion [3].
- Assets on Aladdin: $25 trillion, a different count [4].

## Why are there two trillion-dollar numbers?

$15.5 trillion is what BlackRock manages. $25 trillion is what clients run through Aladdin software, including BlackRock's own [1][4]. Read [blackrock aum vs aladdin assets](/blog/blackrock-aum-vs-aladdin-assets/).

## What does AUM mean?

Assets under management: client money a firm invests for a fee. It isn't the firm's own money. Read [what is aum](/blog/aum/).

## How do you verify the figures?

BlackRock reports AUM every quarter in its earnings release and every year in its 10-K [1][2]. Read [blackrock sec filings](/blog/blackrock-sec-filings/).
