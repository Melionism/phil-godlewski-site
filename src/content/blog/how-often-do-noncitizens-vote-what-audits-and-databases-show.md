All right, guys, this is the question underneath the whole SAVE America Act fight, so let's look at the numbers from audits, including ones run by Republicans. In October 2024, Georgia's Republican Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger announced an audit that found 20 noncitizens among about 8.2 million registered voters; nine had voted, mostly before 2012 [1]. In Kansas, a federal judge found that at most 67 noncitizens had registered or tried to register over 19 years [2]. In New Hampshire, a judge reviewing records from 1998 to 2024 found 47 wrongful-voting cases in about 8.3 million votes, with about eight noncitizens [3]. The federal SAVE database, overhauled in 2025, checked more than 65 million voters in 26 states and flagged 28,635 as potential noncitizens, according to the administration; "potential" matters, because in Texas about 20% of those flagged were later confirmed as citizens [4]. So noncitizen voting happens, it's a crime, and the documented numbers are small.

Here are the audits, databases, what they miss, and public opinion.

**Quick answer**

- How often do noncitizens vote: rarely, by every audit available [1][2][3].
- Georgia 2024: 20 of 8.2 million registered [1].
- SAVE checks: 28,635 flagged of 65M+, before follow-up [4].
- Caution: flags aren't confirmations [4].

## Databases

SAVE, run by DHS, flagged 28,635 potential noncitizens among 65M+ checked voters [4]. In Denton County, Texas, 14 of 84 flagged voters proved citizenship, 14 had checked "not a citizen" on their forms and none had voted, and 55 were canceled for not responding [6]. ## What they miss

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- **Audits find what records show**; undetected cases aren't counted.
- **Flags aren't convictions**; follow-up matters [4].
- **Non-response** can cancel a citizen's registration [6].

## Public opinion

Most Americans think it's worth checking anyway: 66% supported proof of citizenship to register in a March 2026 CBS poll [7]. Why REAL ID may not be enough: [why REAL ID may not be enough](/blog/documents-prove-citizenship-vote/).
