All right, guys, let's talk about the noise. The Trump scandals distraction theory says the big scandals of 2017–2021 pulled the attention of the good guys away at the worst moment. I put it plainly on my July 19, 2026 show: the white hats "were distracted by Russia Russia Russia and impeachment one and impeachment two and Stormy Daniels" [1].

There's a second version, older, that reads the same scandals as decoys on purpose. Here are the real events, with dates, and how each version uses them.

**Quick answer**

- Russia probe: Special Counsel Robert Mueller appointed under DOJ Order No. 3915-2017 [2].
- Impeachment one: House resolution passed December 18, 2019 [3].
- Impeachment two: House resolution passed January 13, 2021 [4].
- The claim: these events distracted or decoyed, depending on the version [1][5].

## How do real events become decoys?

Every one of these events happened. The theory doesn't deny them; it gives them a different job in the story. In one reading, they were a distraction that cost the white hats time [1]. In the other, the white hats used them as cover.

Q pointed in that second direction early. Post 3312, April 11, 2019, used Robert Mueller as an example of a "disinformation push": "Mueller is a WHITE HAT" [5]. For who these people are said to be, see [the people believers see as saviors](/blog/white-hats-conspiracy-theory/).

## What was the Russia investigation?

Here's the paper. DOJ Order No. 3915-2017 named Robert S. Mueller III as Special Counsel [2]. It covered "any links and/or coordination between the Russian government and individuals associated with the campaign" [2].

The case rolled through 2017, 2018 and into 2019. That's more than two years of headlines. In the distraction reading, that's two years when attention went to Russia and not to the 2020 election [1].

## What were the two impeachments?

Do you remember December 18, 2019? I'll give you a hint: H. Res. 755. The House passed it that day, with articles on abuse of power and obstruction of Congress [3].

The second came 7 days before the end of the term. H. Res. 24 passed the House on January 13, 2021, with one article: "Incitement of Insurrection" [4]. It quoted Trump's January 6 speech: "we won this election, and we won it by a landslide" [4].

| Impeachment | Resolution | House vote | Article(s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | H. Res. 755 | Dec 18, 2019 | Abuse of power; obstruction of Congress [3] |
| Second | H. Res. 24 | Jan 13, 2021 | Incitement of insurrection [4] |

## Stormy Daniels and the logic of distraction

The Stormy Daniels story ran from 2018 all the way to a New York trial in 2024. In the distraction reading, it's the clearest case: a personal scandal that ate attention for years [1].

I get it, I get it, it's frustrating to look back on. Here's the logic in one line: while everyone looked at the scandals, the people who should have been ready for 2020 weren't [1]. That's the [claim that the white hats made a mistake](/blog/white-hats-2020-miscalculation/).

The plan version answers differently. Part 8 of devolution says "Biden assuming office had to happen" [6]. In that reading, there was no mistake and no real distraction. See [why nobody blocked the handover of power](/blog/why-white-hats-didnt-stop-biden/).
