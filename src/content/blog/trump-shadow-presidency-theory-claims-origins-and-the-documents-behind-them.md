All right, guys, let's start with the big picture. The Trump shadow presidency theory holds that Donald Trump kept real authority after January 20, 2021, while Joe Biden held the title. The best-known version is devolution, a Substack series Jon Herold (Patel Patriot) began on July 2, 2021 [1]. Its author puts it plainly: "I don't think he ever 'left'" [2].

This page is your map. It covers what the shadow presidency claims, who the white hats are said to be, the alleged shadow cabinet and what the public documents actually say.

**Quick answer**

- What it is: the idea that Trump secretly remained president from 2021 to 2025 [2].
- Main version: devolution, 25 parts and 5 addenda [3].
- Key documents cited: Executive Orders 13848 (2018) and 13961 (2020) [4][5].
- Where the term "shadow government" comes from in the series: Part 13, November 4, 2021 [6].

## What does devolution claim, and where did it come from?

Shadow presidency explained in one line: a legal continuity-of-government mechanism handed power to Trump-aligned people before he left office. Patel Patriot built that case from public documents, starting with a March 2020 Newsweek report on military continuity plans [1].

Part 4, "A Wartime President," published July 18, 2021, goes furthest. It argues Trump may have used a Presidential Emergency Action Document to keep his authority [2]. Read [the core claim believers start from](/devolution-theory/) for the full version.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Trigger | Trump's farewell speech on January 20, 2021 | [1] |
| Legal hook | Executive Order 13961 on continuity, December 7, 2020 | [5][6] |
| Role of Trump | A "wartime president" who never left | [2] |
| Role of Biden | A figurehead in a "fauxnaugurated" administration | [7] |
| End point | Trump's return, "constitutionally sanctioned" | [7] |

## Who are the white hats said to be?

In this theory, white hats are patriots inside the military and the government who work on the plan quietly. The term itself comes from movie westerns, where the good guys wore white hats [8].

Devolution gives the role to the military. Part 9, published August 21, 2021, says the Combatant Commanders "play an integral role" and puts Chris Miller, then Acting Secretary of Defense, at the top [9]. See more on [the secret heroes known as white hats](/white-hats-conspiracy-theory/).

I need you to understand one thing here. Q, the source most people link to white hats, used the phrase in very few posts. The qalerts.app archive shows it in post 3312 (April 11, 2019) and post 4960 (November 7, 2022) [10].



## Who is in the alleged shadow cabinet?

Part 13, "Shadow Government," names the people. It says the Executive Committee created by EO 13961 was the shadow government [6]. The names it lists:

- Christopher Miller, Acting Secretary of Defense;
- Chad Wolf, Acting Secretary of Homeland Security;
- John Ratcliffe, Director of National Intelligence;
- Robert O'Brien, National Security Advisor;
- Russell Vought, Tony Ornato and Kevin Droegemeier;
- Mark Meadows as a "back channel" to Trump.

Part 13 also says it is "no longer a question of if devolution is real, but rather when and how it is revealed" [6]. You can check [the officials most often mentioned](/trump-shadow-cabinet-theory/) one by one on our page about them.

## What did Trump say on January 20, 2021?

Do you remember where it all started? Joint Base Andrews, the morning of January 20, 2021. Trump's departure remarks are on the official record at govinfo.gov [14]. Three lines from that speech get quoted in this community more than any others:

- "We will be back in some form" [14].
- "I will always fight for you" [14].
- "I wish the new administration great luck and great success" [14].

Believers read the first line as a signal. Patel Patriot says the speech left him confused that day, and that is where the series begins [1].

## What happened after the 2024 election?

The story turned a corner in January 2025. On January 19, 2025, EO 14146 revoked most of EO 13961 [13]. On January 20, 2025, Trump gave his second inaugural address: "the golden age of America begins right now" [17].

The theory's own end point was always Trump's return, and Patel Patriot said it would have to be "constitutionally sanctioned" [7]. How each version reads that return is a story we keep following.



## What do the documents show?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Here is what each key document says in its own text.

| Document | Date | What the text says | How the theory reads it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EO 13848 | Sep 12, 2018 | Within 45 days of an election, the DNI assesses foreign interference [4] | The trigger for action after 2020 |
| EO 13961 | Dec 7, 2020 | Creates a Federal Mission Resilience Executive Committee [5] | The shadow government [6] |
| Biden notice | Sep 7, 2021 | Continues the EO 13848 national emergency for 1 year [11] | Proof the order still mattered |
| House Doc. 118-165 | Sep 9, 2024 | Continues it again beyond September 12, 2024 [12] | Same |
| EO 14146 | Jan 19, 2025 | Revokes sections 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7 of EO 13961 [13] | — |

Here's why EO 13848 matters to believers. Section 1 gives the Director of National Intelligence 45 days after an election to assess foreign interference. The Attorney General and Homeland Security then have 45 days from that assessment to report [4]. For the November 3, 2020 election, the first window closed on December 18, 2020.

The word "devolution" does not appear in EO 13961 [5]. The link comes from the author's reading. Ask the bigger question too: [whether a shadow presidency is realistic in the US](/can-shadow-president-exist/).
