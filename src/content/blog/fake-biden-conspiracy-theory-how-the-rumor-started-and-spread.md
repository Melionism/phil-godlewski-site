All right, guys, this is one of the most talked-about stories in the whole community. The fake Biden conspiracy theory says the man who served as president from 2021 to 2025 was not the real Joe Biden, or was only playing a part. Patel Patriot wrote that Biden was "fauxnaugurated" and is "on a set replica of the WH almost always" [1]. On my July 19, 2026 show I asked what Biden would do "if Joe Biden were the real Joe Biden" and called the whole thing "a massive central casting. It's a movie" [2].

The rumor got a lift at the end of May 2025, when Trump reshared a post on Truth Social claiming Biden had been executed in 2020 and replaced by clones. Then, in June 2025, a White House memorandum raised questions about who really signed Biden's orders [3]. Here's how each piece of the fake Biden theory fits.

**Quick answer**

- The claim: masks, clones, body doubles or an actor in place of Biden [1][2].
- Staged inauguration: Patel Patriot's "fauxnaugurated" [1].
- Official questions: a June 4, 2025 memorandum on the autopen and Biden's "cognitive decline" [3].
- House report: "The Biden Autopen Presidency," October 2025 [4].

## The 'staged' inauguration

In "Does Devolution Require Trump's Return?", Patel Patriot uses two words that stuck: "fauxnaugurated" and the "byeden administration" [1]. In his reading, the inauguration on January 20, 2021 was a show.

The public record for that morning shows Trump at Joint Base Andrews saying: "I wish the new administration great luck and great success" [5]. He also said: "We will be back in some form" [5]. Believers read the second line as the real message. Ask yourself: [was biden inauguration fake](/blog/biden-inauguration-fake/)? We look at every staging claim there.

## Legitimate questions about who led the administration

Here's where the story meets official documents. On June 4, 2025, a presidential memorandum stated: "For years, President Biden suffered from serious cognitive decline" [3]. It also said: "The vast majority of Biden's executive actions were signed using a mechanical signature pen, often called an autopen" [3]. It ordered the Counsel to the President to investigate [3].

In October 2025, the House Oversight Committee released "The Biden Autopen Presidency: Decline, Delusion, and Deception in the White House" [4]. Chairman James Comer said: "This is a massive cover-up" [4]. He also asked the Attorney General to review Biden-era executive actions [4].

I need you to understand the difference here. These documents ask who made decisions in Biden's name. They don't say Biden was replaced by someone else [3][4]. Read more on [legitimate questions about power in the Biden White House](/blog/who-running-biden-administration/).

## How do you assess the rumor?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Four questions:

1. **Which version are you looking at?** Mask, clone, actor, or aides in control?
2. **Is there a document?** The memo and the House report exist; a clone document doesn't [3][4].
3. **Who said it, and when?** Note the date and the source of every post you share.
