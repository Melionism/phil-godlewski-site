John Solomon is a longtime conservative investigative journalist, founder of the outlet Just the News, who joined the White House's election task force in 2026. He's on our side of this fight. Asked whether the 2020 results were valid, he said he was "still researching" [1]. That's an honest statement from an ally, and it tells you exactly where the evidence stands.
