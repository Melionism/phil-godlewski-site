All right, guys, risk is the heart of Aladdin. Aladdin risk management means measuring how a portfolio could lose money, in numbers, every day. Central Banking's 2022 review lists what it offers: daily transparency into positions, exposures, performance and attribution, portfolio risk, scenario analysis and compliance, with metrics such as "duration, convexity, value-at-risk, and credit risk measures" [1]. BlackRock's 10-K says its edge comes from combining "risk analytics with portfolio management" [2].

Here's why risk comes first, the stress tests, the compliance checks, the metrics that matter and how this compares with other systems.

**Quick answer**

- Aladdin risk metrics: value-at-risk, duration, convexity, credit risk [1].
- Aladdin factor models: break risk into drivers like rates, credit and currencies.
- Scenarios: stress tests on historical and made-up shocks [1].
- How Aladdin measures risk: every position, in one system, daily [1].

## Why does risk come first?

Because the name says so: assets, liabilities, debt and derivatives [3]. A fund can't manage what it can't measure. For the overview, see [what is blackrock aladdin](/blog/blackrock-aladdin/).

## What about stress tests and scenarios?

Aladdin can shock a portfolio and see what breaks. The Bank of Israel shocked covariance matrices and volatility to study Russia–Ukraine risk, using both long-term and 2014-based correlations [1]. See [aladdin stress testing](/blog/aladdin-stress-testing/).

## How do trading and compliance checks fit?

The same system that measures risk also checks the rules before a trade goes out. That's [order management within Aladdin](/blog/aladdin-trading-compliance/).

## Which metrics matter?

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Value-at-risk (VaR) | Expected worst loss over a period, at a set confidence |
| Duration | How much bond prices move when rates change |
| Convexity | How duration itself changes as rates move |
| Credit risk | Exposure to borrowers who may default |
| Factor exposure | How much risk comes from rates, credit, equity, currency |

These are the analytics listed for Aladdin in Central Banking's review [1].

## How does this compare with other systems?

I need you to know Aladdin isn't the only one. BlackRock's 10-K says it competes with other risk analytics providers, some with "more sophisticated offerings" in places [2]. See [who else plays in this market](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-competitors/).

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Metrics measure the past and model the future. They don't promise it.

## FAQ
