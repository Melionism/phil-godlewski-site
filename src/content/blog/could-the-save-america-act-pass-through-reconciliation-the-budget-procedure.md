All right, guys, when the filibuster blocked the SAVE America Act, a lot of people said, "Just put it in a budget bill." That's reconciliation, and here's why it didn't work. Reconciliation is a fast-track process for spending and revenue changes that carry out a budget resolution; debate is limited, so no filibuster and no 60-vote cloture [1]. But the Byrd rule, adopted in 1986, bars "extraneous" matter from reconciliation bills [1]. A provision is extraneous if, among other tests, it produces no change in spending or revenue, or if its budget effects are "merely incidental" to its policy purpose [1]. Waiving the rule takes 60 votes [1]. Voter ID and proof of citizenship are election policy, not budget policy. Reconciliation provisions must be principally about the budget rather than policy [4]. When Republicans tried to add it to the party-line immigration enforcement funding bill on April 23, 2026, it failed 48-50, with four Republicans voting no [3].

Here's what reconciliation is, the Byrd rule, why it's hard, and other routes.

**Quick answer**

- SAVE America Act reconciliation: blocked by the Byrd rule on non-budget matter [1][2].
- Test: no budget effect, or effect "merely incidental," means extraneous [1].
- Waiver: 60 votes [1].
- Result: April 23, 2026 attempt failed 48-50 [3].

## The Byrd rule

Six tests make matter extraneous [1]:

1. No change in outlays or revenue.
2. Increases deficit while the committee misses its target.
3. Outside the committee's jurisdiction.
4. Budget effect "merely incidental."
5. Adds deficits beyond the budget window.
6. Changes Social Security.

## Why it's hard

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- **Election rules don't spend or raise money** in a meaningful way.
- **The parliamentarian advises** the presiding officer; ignoring her takes a vote many senators won't cast [1][4].
- **Four Republicans** voted against the June attempt [3].

## Other routes

1. **Regular order with 60 votes**: failed in 2026 [2].
2. **Ending the filibuster**: no 51 votes [5].
3. **A fall reconciliation package** with defense spending and voter ID elements: planned, then delayed [2].
4. **State laws**: several states passed proof-of-citizenship laws in 2025 [6].

## FAQ

**Could the SAVE America Act pass through reconciliation?** Not under the Byrd rule as applied [1][2].
