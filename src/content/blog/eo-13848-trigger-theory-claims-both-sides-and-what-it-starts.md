All right, guys, I know a lot of you held on to "13848" through the winter of 2020. The idea was that this order was the trigger: once the intelligence report confirmed foreign interference, Trump could freeze assets, arrest traitors, bring in the military, even reverse the election. I understand why it caught on. It was a real order, signed by Trump, about foreign election interference, with a deadline on December 18, 2020. But I'm going to be straight with you about what the text actually starts. EO 13848 triggers assessments, reports and sanctions on foreign persons. Its tools are blocking property, banning entry, and cutting off transactions [1]. It doesn't mention the military, arrests, tribunals, ballots, electors or re-running an election [1]. In December 2020, Snopes rated a viral claim that Trump would "tactically" implement it, with patrols and seized social media assets, as false [2].

Here's where the idea came from, the house and passport claims, treason and arrests, the real steps from election to sanctions, supporters' arguments and lawyers' objections, what happened after 2020 and why it wasn't used, and the military question with ten myths.

**Quick answer**

- EO 13848 trigger theory: the belief that the order would unlock arrests, military action or a reversed 2020 result.
- What it really starts: a DNI assessment, an AG-DHS report and possible Treasury sanctions on foreign persons [1].
- What it doesn't contain: military, arrests, tribunals, ballot seizures [1].
- 2020 outcome: assessments found no foreign alteration of votes; no domestic action followed [3][4].

## Where the idea came from

In November and December 2020, posts and articles connected three things: claims of foreign involvement in voting machines, the order's 45-day deadline, and the order's broad wording. A December 18, 2020 American Thinker piece argued the AG-DHS report could recommend that Vice President Pence accept alternate electors [5]. The order in plain English: [eo 13848 in plain english](/blog/eo-13848-complete-guide/).

## House and passport claims

The claim: the order lets the government take your home or passport. The text blocks property of designated foreign persons and suspends their entry [1]. Details: [can eo 13848 take your house](/blog/can-eo-13848-take-your-house/).

## Treason and arrests

Sanctions are civil and administrative. Treason is a crime defined in the Constitution, prosecuted in court. More: [what the Constitution says about treason](/blog/eo-13848-treason/).

## The real steps from election to sanctions

1. **Election day.**
2. **DNI assessment** within 45 days [1].
3. **AG-DHS report** within 45 days of that [1].
4. **Treasury review** and possible blocking of foreign persons [1].
5. **State-Treasury recommendation** on broader sanctions [1].

There's no step six that turns into arrests or a new election.

## Supporters' arguments and lawyers' objections

| Supporters argue | Lawyers object |
| --- | --- |
| Foreign interference was real | Yes, but the order targets foreigners [1] |
| "Any person" can be sanctioned | Section 2 says "foreign person" [1] |
| Sanctions can hit those who helped | Americans face liability for dealing with blocked persons, not under Section 2 itself [1] |
| The report could recommend remedies | Remedies are "other than sanctions," e.g., security fixes [1] |
| The emergency gives big powers | IEEPA powers are economic, with exceptions [6] |

## What happened after 2020 and why it wasn't used

The DNI's assessment, dated March 10, 2021, found "no indications that any foreign actor attempted to alter any technical aspect of the voting process" [3]. DOJ and DHS found claims of foreign control of infrastructure "not credible" [4]. Treasury did sanction Andrii Derkach's network in January 2021 for influence operations, under this order [7]. An unsigned December 16, 2020 draft order to have the Defense Secretary seize voting machines was never issued [8].

## The military question and ten myths

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

The order never mentions the armed forces [1]. Ten myths, checked against the text:

1. It authorizes martial law. **No mention** [1].
2. It allows military tribunals. **No mention** [1].
3. It lets Trump arrest people. **No arrest power** [1].
4. It overturns elections. **No such step** [1].
5. It seizes ballots or machines. **No mention** [1].
6. It targets Americans. **Section 2 covers foreign persons** [1].
7. It takes houses. **Blocks, doesn't take** [1].
8. It's secret. **Published in the Federal Register** [1].
9. Biden renewing it proves it was used against him. **Renewal is routine** [9].
10. The deadline passing means it's "in motion." **The 2020 reports are public** [3][4].
