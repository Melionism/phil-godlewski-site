All right, guys, before we talk resets and collapses, we need the map. How the financial system works comes down to four parts. The central bank sets the price of money. Banks hold deposits and make loans. Markets trade stocks and bonds. Payment systems move money between all of them. In the US the central bank is the Federal Reserve, created by Congress [1]. Congress writes the rules; the Fed, Treasury and regulators carry them out.

Here are the main parts, who can change the rules, AI in the machinery of finance and how the parts connect.

**Quick answer**

- Financial system explained: central bank, banks, markets, payments.
- How banks and central banks work: banks lend; the Fed sets rates and supplies reserves [1].
- US financial system basics: Congress makes the laws, agencies apply them.
- Payments: the Fed runs FedNow, live since July 20, 2023 [2].

## The main parts of the system

| Part | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |

Congress passes the laws. The Fed sets monetary policy. Treasury manages federal finances. Bank regulators supervise. Read [who controls the us financial system](/blog/who-controls-us-financial-system/).

## AI in the machinery of finance

AI already runs inside the system. Treasury's December 2024 report lists fraud detection, credit underwriting, customer service and trading [3]. Read [how banks actually use artificial intelligence](/blog/ai-finance/).

## How do the parts connect?

I need you to follow one dollar:

1. **You deposit** a paycheck at your bank.
2. **The bank lends** part of the money to someone else.
3. **The bank holds reserves** at the Fed and settles payments through it [1].
4. **Markets** let the bank and you buy bonds or stocks.

When banks lend, they create new deposits. Read [how the fed creates money](/blog/fed-creates-money/).
