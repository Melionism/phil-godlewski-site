All right, guys, what did Pompeo do after 2021? The public record is clear on the big moves. He signed with Fox News as a contributor on April 8, 2021 [1]. He became a distinguished fellow at the Hudson Institute [2]. He visited Taiwan on March 2–5, 2022 [2]. And on April 14, 2023, he said he would not run for president in 2024 [3].

That's Pompeo after leaving office in four lines. Below: his roles, his foreign meetings and how to tell a private trip from an official one.

- Media: Fox News contributor from April 2021 [1].
- Think tank: Hudson Institute distinguished fellow [2].
- Pompeo foreign meetings: Taiwan, March 2022, hosted by the Prospect Foundation [2].
- Politics: no 2024 run, announced April 14, 2023 [3].

## Which meetings abroad did he have?

The best-known one is Taiwan. The Taipei-based Prospect Foundation invited him, and Taiwan's Foreign Ministry gave the dates as March 2–5, 2022 [2]. He was scheduled to meet President Tsai Ing-wen on March 3 [2].

Beijing reacted. Its spokesman said: "Pompeo has long been sanctioned by China due to his anti-China actions and deeds" [2]. Those sanctions date to January 20, 2021, the last day of his term, and bar him from entering the mainland, Hong Kong and Macao [4].

## How do you tell a private trip from an official one?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Four signs:

1. **Who invited him?** A foundation or a government? Taiwan 2022 was the Prospect Foundation [2].
2. **Was it on the State Department schedule?** Official trips are announced by the Department, like the cancelled January 2021 Europe trip [7].
3. **Is there a FARA filing?** Acting for a foreign government requires one [8].
4. **Does the timing fit § 207?** Senior ex-officials face 1- and 2-year limits [6].
