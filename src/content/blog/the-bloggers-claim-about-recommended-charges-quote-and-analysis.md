All right, guys, after the July 16 address, a claim spread on livestreams and posts: Trump "recommended charges" against Obama. I want to be fair to everyone here, so let's take it apart carefully. The transcript of the address has Obama's name once, in a line about "burn bags" of documents [1]. It contains a general instruction to investigate and prosecute "those responsible for any crimes," but no named target and no recommended charge against Obama [1]. There is a real referral in the background, though, and that's probably where the confusion comes from: in July 2025, DNI Tulsi Gabbard said she was sending documents about the 2016 Russia intelligence assessment to the Justice Department and FBI for a criminal referral, and Trump called the conduct "treason" [2]. That's about 2016, not 2020.

Here's the exact quote, what's claimed, what's known and the conclusion.

**Quick answer**

- Obama criminal referral claim: the July 2026 speech didn't recommend charges against Obama [1].
- Blogger Obama charges claim: likely mixes up the speech with Gabbard's 2025 referral about 2016 [2].
- Obama DOJ referral: Gabbard referred 2016 intelligence documents in July 2025 [2].
- Status: no charges against Obama have been announced.
