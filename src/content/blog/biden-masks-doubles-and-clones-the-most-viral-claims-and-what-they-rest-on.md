All right, guys, let's go through the Biden clone theory piece by piece. It comes in three forms: a mask, a body double, or a clone. Its biggest moment came on May 31, 2025, when Trump reshared a post that read: "There is no #JoeBiden - executed in 2020" and "#Biden clones doubles & robotic engineered soulless mindless entities are what you see."

Before that, Patel Patriot had written that Biden is "on a set replica of the WH almost always" [1]. Here are the most viral claims, how they spread and why photos can fool you.

**Quick answer**

- Biden mask claim: photos said to show a mask line or different features.
- Biden body double: a stand-in playing the role [1][2].
- Biden clone conspiracy: the May 2025 post Trump reshared.
- Biden double photos: side-by-side images comparing ears, teeth or height.

## Why do images mislead?

Because photos change with everything around them. Here's what changes a face in a picture:

1. **The lens.** A wide lens up close makes a nose and ears look bigger.
2. **The light.** Hard light from above changes the shape of a face.
3. **Compression.** Reposted images lose detail and gain blocky edges that look like seams.
4. **Age.** Photos years apart show a different person in every case.

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Find the original photo, its date and who took it, before you compare. The film-set idea comes up here too: see [the real company called Central Casting](/blog/central-casting-meaning-conspiracy/).
