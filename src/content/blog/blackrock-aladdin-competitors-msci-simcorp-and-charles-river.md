All right, guys, Aladdin has rivals, and some are owned by giants. The main BlackRock Aladdin competitors include MSCI, SimCorp, now owned by Deutsche Börse after a 2023 takeover [1], and Charles River, which State Street bought on October 1, 2018 [2]. Bloomberg's PORT and Addepar compete in parts of the market. BlackRock's 10-K says Aladdin faces competitors "with more sophisticated offerings" in some areas [3].

Here's how the market is divided, and how each rival lines up.

**Quick answer**

- Aladdin vs SimCorp: SimCorp is Deutsche Börse's since 2023 [1].
- Aladdin vs Charles River: Charles River is State Street's since 2018 [2].
- Aladdin vs MSCI: MSCI is known for indexes and risk analytics.
- Other Aladdin alternatives: Bloomberg PORT, Addepar and in-house builds [3].

## Aladdin and Bloomberg PORT

PORT lives inside the Bloomberg Terminal and focuses on portfolio and risk analytics. Aladdin aims wider, from risk to trading to operations [3]. Read [how Bloomberg's tool differs from Aladdin](/blog/aladdin-vs-bloomberg-port/).

## Aladdin and Addepar

Addepar is built mostly for wealth managers and family offices to see all their holdings in one place. It meets Aladdin Wealth in that market. Read [aladdin vs addepar](/blog/aladdin-vs-addepar/).

I need you to notice who owns them: a stock exchange group and one of the biggest custody banks [1][2]. Aladdin's rivals aren't small shops. That's one reason Aladdin isn't the only "operating system" of finance.
