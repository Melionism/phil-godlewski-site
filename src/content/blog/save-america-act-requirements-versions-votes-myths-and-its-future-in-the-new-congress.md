All right, guys, the SAVE America Act was the biggest election bill of 2026, and a lot of what's said about it, on both sides, is off. Here's what the House-passed version requires. To register for federal elections, you'd have to show documentary proof of U.S. citizenship, such as a passport, or a birth record together with a government photo ID or military ID [1]. A REAL ID counts only if it "indicates the applicant is a citizen," and most states' REAL IDs don't [2][3]. You'd need a photo ID to vote in person; without one you could cast a provisional ballot and show ID within three days [1]. Mail voters would send a copy of their ID, or the last four digits of their Social Security number with an affidavit [1]. Within 30 days, states would send their voter lists to DHS to check against the SAVE database [1]. Election officials who register someone without the required proof could face criminal penalties [1][2]. The House passed it 218-213 in February 2026; it never got 60 votes in the Senate [2][4].

Here are the arguments on both sides, documents required, implementation, and versions, votes, myths and the new Congress.

**Quick answer**

- SAVE America Act requirements: proof of citizenship to register, photo ID to vote, ID for mail ballots, voter list checks through DHS [1].
- Documents: passport, or birth record plus photo ID; REAL ID only if it shows citizenship [1][2].
- Votes: House 218-213 in February 2026; Senate never reached 60 [2][4].
- Future: with Democrats taking Congress in January 2027, its chances drop sharply [5].

## Arguments on both sides

Supporters say it guarantees only citizens vote and has broad public support; a CBS poll in March 2026 found 80% backed photo ID to vote [6]. Opponents say millions of citizens lack easy access to documents [7]. The full debate: [save america act pros and cons](/blog/save-america-act-pros-cons/).

## Implementation

The bill would take effect immediately and includes no new funding, according to election officials; Orange County, California, estimated it would need 59 more staff and about $6 million to verify its 2024 registrants [8]. ## FAQ

**What does the SAVE America Act require?** Proof of citizenship to register and photo ID to vote [1].

**Is a driver's license enough?** Only for photo ID at the polls; not as proof of citizenship unless it shows citizenship [1][2].

**Did it pass?** The House, yes; the Senate, no [2][4].

**Does it affect mail voting?** It requires ID copies with mail ballots [1].

**What happens now?** A Democratic Congress is unlikely to take it up [5].
