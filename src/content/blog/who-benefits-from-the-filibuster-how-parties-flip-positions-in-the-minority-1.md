All right, guys, here's something to keep in mind as 2027 starts. Both parties love the filibuster when they're in the minority and hate it when they're in the majority. In January 2022, Democrats held the Senate and tried to carve out an exception for their voting bills; it failed 52-48 when Sens. Joe Manchin and Kyrsten Sinema joined all 50 Republicans [1]. In 2026, Republicans held the Senate and the roles reversed: Trump pushed to end the filibuster for the SAVE America Act, and Majority Leader John Thune said Republicans didn't have the 51 votes [2]. Trump warned that Democrats would end it "in the first hour back" if they regained power [2]. Now they have: AP reported Democrats won the Senate in November 2026 [3]. Starting January 3, 2027, Republicans will be the minority, and the filibuster will be their main tool to block Democratic bills.

Here's the majority, the minority, past reversals, and what's next.

**Quick answer**

- Who benefits from the filibuster: whichever party is in the minority.
- 2022: Republicans used it to block Democratic voting bills [1].
- 2026: Democrats used it to block the SAVE America Act [2].
- 2027: Republicans become the minority and gain the filibuster's protection [3].
