All right, guys, let's put names on it. The Trump shadow cabinet theory says a team of loyal officials kept working for Trump after January 20, 2021. Devolution Part 13, published November 4, 2021, names them: Christopher Miller, Chad Wolf, John Ratcliffe, Robert O'Brien, Russell Vought, Tony Ornato and Kevin Droegemeier, with Mark Meadows as a "back channel" [1].

Other names come up in the community too: Mike Pompeo, Kash Patel, Devin Nunes, Dan Scavino. Here is what each one's public record shows about Trump officials after 2021.

**Quick answer**

- Source of the list: Part 13, "Shadow Government" [1].
- Legal hook: the Executive Committee created by EO 13961 [1][2].
- Back in government by 2025: Ratcliffe at the CIA, Patel at the FBI [3][4].
- Rules that apply abroad: 18 U.S.C. § 207, FARA and the Logan Act [5][6][7].

## Why does the theory need a hidden team?

Because a shadow presidency needs people. Part 13 says the Executive Committee created by EO 13961 on December 7, 2020 was the shadow government [1][2]. It also says these officials later joined groups like the Center for Renewing America, the America First Policy Institute and Boundary Channel Partners [1]. This is [where this whole story begins](/blog/trump-shadow-presidency-theory/).

## Pompeo's travel claims

A claim goes around that Pompeo went to China after the 2020 election. The official record shows his last trip as Secretary was a seven-nation tour that began in Paris on November 14, 2020: France, Turkey, Georgia, Israel, UAE, Qatar and Saudi Arabia [8]. See [Pompeo's last foreign trips](/blog/pompeo-visit-china-after-2020-election/) in detail.

## Rules for former officials abroad

Former officials can travel, speak and do business. But there are rules. Under 18 U.S.C. § 207(f), for one year after leaving, senior officials can't represent a foreign government to influence US decisions [5]. FARA, enacted in 1938, requires agents of foreign principals to register and disclose [6]. Read [former us officials foreign travel rules](/blog/former-us-officials-foreign-travel-rules/).

## Pompeo after 2021

He joined Fox News as a contributor on April 8, 2021 [9]. He visited Taiwan March 2–5, 2022, as a Hudson Institute distinguished fellow [10]. On April 14, 2023, he said he would not run for president in 2024 [11]. See [Pompeo's meetings after January 2021](/blog/pompeo-after-leaving-office/).

## John Ratcliffe

DNI from 2020 to 2021, then outside government: senior adviser at American Global Strategies, co-chair at the America First Policy Institute, and from June 2023 a Heritage Foundation visiting fellow [12][13]. On January 23, 2025, the Senate confirmed him as CIA Director, 74–25 [3]. Read [what he did after 2021](/blog/john-ratcliffe-after-dni/).

## Patel and Nunes

Kash Patel was confirmed as FBI Director on February 20, 2025, by a 51–49 vote [4]. Devin Nunes left Congress after announcing his retirement on December 6, 2021, the same day Trump named him to lead Trump Media and Truth Social [14]. He joined the President's Intelligence Advisory Board in January 2025 [14]. See [why these two were chosen](/blog/kash-patel-devin-nunes-shadow-government/).

## Dan Scavino's posts

Q linked to Scavino's tweets in at least three posts, starting February 16, 2019 [15]. In October 2025, Trump named him to lead the White House Presidential Personnel Office while keeping his Deputy Chief of Staff title [16]. Read about [dan scavino posts secret signals](/blog/dan-scavino-posts-secret-signals/).

## The Logan Act and private diplomacy

The Logan Act of 1799 bars citizens from dealing with foreign governments to defeat US policy without authority [7]. Only two people were ever indicted, and no one has been convicted [7]. Read [logan act explained](/blog/logan-act-explained/), and see how the [presidential transfer of power](/blog/presidential-transfer-power/) works in law.
