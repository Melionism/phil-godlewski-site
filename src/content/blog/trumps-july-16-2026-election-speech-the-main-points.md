All right, guys, on Thursday night, July 16, 2026, President Trump gave a 27-minute primetime address from the White House on election security [1]. A lot of us had waited years for this. He said he had declassified documents on election vulnerabilities, Chinese interference and fraud, and that he was ordering investigations into how that information was "hidden" [2][3]. He said China made "the largest compromise of election data in history," citing 220 million U.S. voter files [3]. He cited a Homeland Security review claiming about 278,000 noncitizens were registered to vote in four states [2]. He called mail-in ballots "inherently corrupt," pointed to a 2020 voter registration case in Muskegon, Michigan, and spent the last two minutes urging Congress to pass the SAVE America Act [1][4]. He closed with: "If there can be no trust, there can be no greatness" [3].

What he did not do: present evidence that votes were changed or that the 2020 outcome was altered, according to ABC News and other outlets that reviewed the speech and documents [3][5]. Here's the released documents, the reactions, the "25 percent" claim and what's next.

**Quick answer**

- Trump July 16 election speech: a 27-minute primetime address on election security [1].
- Main claims: China obtained 220 million voter files; 278,000 noncitizens registered; mail ballots "inherently corrupt"; information was hidden [2][4].
- What he ordered: investigations by the DNI, DOJ, FBI and CIA into how the intelligence was handled, "including potential criminal charges" [2].
- What wasn't shown: evidence that votes were flipped in 2020 [3][5].
