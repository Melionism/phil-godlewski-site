All right, guys, here's where EO 13848 can actually touch Americans. It's not by making them targets under Section 2. It's by what they do with people who are. Once Treasury designates a foreign person, U.S. persons are barred from dealing in that person's property, from making donations to them, and from providing or receiving "any contribution or provision of funds, goods, or services" to, from or for their benefit [1]. Evading or conspiring to evade those rules is also prohibited [1]. The penalty for a violation can be a civil fine of up to $250,000 or twice the value of the transaction, whichever is greater, adjusted for inflation; a willful violation can bring up to $1 million in fines and 20 years in prison [2]. A real example: Treasury's December 2024 action said the director of Russia's Center for Geopolitical Expertise coordinated GRU funding for its employees and for "U.S.-based facilitators" [3].

Here's how liability arises, US companies, bank accounts and donations.

**Quick answer**

- Americans dealing with blocked persons: prohibited by Sections 4, 5 and 7 of EO 13848 [1].
- Civil penalty: up to $250,000 or twice the transaction, inflation-adjusted [2].
- Criminal penalty: up to $1 million and 20 years, if willful [2].
- Banks and companies: must freeze blocked property and report it [4].

## How liability arises

| Act | Prohibited? |
| --- | --- |
| Paying a blocked person | Yes [1] |
| Getting paid by a blocked person | Yes [1] |
| Donating to one | Yes [1] |
| Helping someone evade | Yes [1] |
| Reading or reporting their content | Not a transaction; informational materials are protected [5] |

What foreign person means in the text: [what foreign person means in the text](/blog/eo-13848-apply-us-citizens/).

## US companies

Companies, including foreign branches of U.S. firms, must not deal with blocked persons or entities they own [1][4]. Many screen customers against the SDN list.

## Bank accounts

A bank holding a blocked person's property must freeze it and report it to OFAC [4]. A license can allow specific transactions, for example certain legal services [4].

## Donations

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- Donations of articles to blocked persons are banned [1].
- Foreign nationals can't give to U.S. campaigns anyway [6].
- If a donation has no foreign link, the order doesn't apply: [why the order doesn't reach purely domestic cases](/blog/foreign-interference-vs-domestic-election-fraud/).

Allegations against specific people are not established facts unless proven in court.

## FAQ

**Can Americans be liable under EO 13848?** Yes, for dealing with blocked persons [1].

**What's the civil penalty?** Up to $250,000 or twice the transaction [2].

**What's the criminal penalty?** Up to $1 million and 20 years, if willful [2].

**Do banks freeze blocked funds?** Yes, and report them [4].

**Is talking to a blocked person illegal?** Personal communications without value transferred are exempt under IEEPA [5].
