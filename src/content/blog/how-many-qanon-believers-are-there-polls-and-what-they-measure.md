All right, guys, you'll hear numbers from "a tiny fringe" to "tens of millions." Both sides pick the number they like. Here's what the polls actually measured. PRRI's March 2021 survey of 5,625 adults didn't ask "Do you believe in QAnon?" It asked about three statements [1]. Fifteen percent agreed that the government, media and financial worlds are controlled by "Satan-worshipping pedophiles who run a global child sex trafficking operation" [1]. Twenty percent agreed that "There is a storm coming soon that will sweep away the elites in power and restore the rightful leaders" [1]. Fifteen percent agreed that "true American patriots may have to resort to violence" [1]. Combining them, PRRI counted 14% as believers, 46% as doubters who didn't fully reject the ideas, and 40% as rejecters [1]. Among Republicans, 23% were believers; among independents, 12%; among Democrats, 7% [1]. Outside the U.S., a 2022 German study found 12.4% agreeing at least partly with core QAnon narratives [2]. Numbers grew during the 2020 lockdowns [3].

Here are the claims, the pandemic, other countries, and poll wording.

**Quick answer**

- How many QAnon believers: 14% of Americans in PRRI's 2021 survey [1].
- Doubters: 46%; rejecters: 40% [1].
- By party: Republicans 23%, independents 12%, Democrats 7% [1].
- Germany: 12.4% partial agreement in 2022 [2].
