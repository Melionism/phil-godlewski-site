All right, guys, drop 34 didn't only predict an arrest. It said "Public riots are being organized in serious numbers" [1]. So let's look at what actually happened in November 2017. In the weeks before, a separate rumor had spread online that "Antifa" was planning to start a civil war on November 4, 2017. PBS reported that Google's "Top stories" boxes even showed headlines repeating the claim in early October [2]. TIME was blunt on November 3: "To be blunt, that's not true. At all" [3]. The real event was a set of anti-Trump rallies organized by Refuse Fascism in about 20 cities, and the group's organizers called the civil-war claims "lies through and through" [2][3]. TIME noted that "Antifa" isn't a single organization but a loose confederation, and that just 990 people had RSVP'd for the New York event on Facebook [3]. On the day, about 300 people showed up in New York and dozens in cities like San Francisco, Los Angeles, Austin and Portland. The marches largely passed without incident; police broke up one brief altercation [4]. No riots "in serious numbers" happened, and no military control followed.

Here's the claim, the Antifa rumor, what happened, and lessons.
