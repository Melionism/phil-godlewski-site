All right, guys, every time an app pops up "Allow access to your location?", you're making a privacy decision. Most of us tap "Allow" without thinking. Here are the dangerous app permissions that really matter, and how to check them on Android and iPhone. The big ones are location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos and tracking. Location is the most sensitive: the FTC found that the data broker X-Mode collected location data through code inside other companies' apps and sold it tied to advertising IDs, including visits to medical clinics and places of worship [1].

Here are the permissions that matter, smart devices, location, voice assistants and how to review them.

**Quick answer**

- Which app permissions to deny: location (when not needed), microphone, contacts, full photo access, tracking.
- Android app permissions: Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Permission manager [2].
- iPhone app permissions: Settings > Privacy & Security; plus "Ask App Not to Track."
- Rule: give each app the least access it needs.

## Permissions that matter

| Permission | Why it's sensitive | Safer choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Reveals home, work, clinics, routines [1] | "While using" or "Never"; turn off precise |
| Microphone | Can record audio | Only for calls and voice apps |
| Camera | Photos and video | Only when needed |
| Contacts | Exposes friends and family too | Rarely needed |
| Photos | Location tags, faces, documents | "Selected photos" only |
| Tracking (iPhone) / Ad ID (Android) | Links activity across apps | Ask not to track / delete ad ID |
| Accessibility (Android) | Can read the screen | Only trusted apps |

## How to review them

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

**Android:** Google's help page explains the Permission manager, where you can see which apps use each permission and change it [2]. Android also removes permissions from apps you haven't used in a while.

**iPhone:** Settings > Privacy & Security shows each permission and the apps using it. Turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" to block tracking requests across the board.

A 10-minute routine:

1. **Open location** and set most apps to "While using" or "Never."
2. **Turn off precise location** for apps that only need your city.
3. **Check microphone and camera** lists for surprises.
4. **Remove contact access** from apps that don't need it.
5. **Delete apps** you haven't opened in months.

For the full phone setup, read [phone privacy settings](/blog/phone-privacy-settings/).

## A closer look: why "free" apps want so much

A flashlight app doesn't need your contacts. A weather app doesn't need your location every minute. When a free app asks for more than its job needs, that extra data is often what pays for it. The X-Mode case showed how location gathered inside ordinary apps can end up sold to others [1].

## FAQ

**Which app permissions should I deny?** Location, microphone, contacts and tracking for apps that don't need them.

**Where are Android app permissions?** In the Permission manager [2].

**Where are iPhone app permissions?** Settings > Privacy & Security.

**Is location the most sensitive?** Usually, yes [1].

**Should I delete unused apps?** Yes; fewer apps, fewer permissions.
