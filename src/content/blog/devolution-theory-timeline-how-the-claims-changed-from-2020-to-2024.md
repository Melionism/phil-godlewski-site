## 2022–2023: expansion and new promises

The series grew wider. Parts 16 to 23 came out in 2022, with titles like "To Xi, or not to Xi?" and "2000 Mules" [3][7]. The Introduction, posted April 6, 2022, became the entry point for new readers [9].

The question of the return got a new answer. In "Does Devolution Require Trump's Return?", listed in the 2022 archive, Patel Patriot says any return "will have to be constitutionally sanctioned" [7][17]. Power Hour episode #98 covered the 2022 midterms [7].

I get it, I get it. A lot of us wanted it to end in 2021. Look how the wording changed: from "imminent" in 2021 to "constitutionally sanctioned" and a warning about PEADs by 2023 [5][17][19].

## 2024 and what came after

On September 9, 2024, Biden continued the EO 13848 national emergency again, beyond September 12, 2024 [10]. Then the story changed course. On January 19, 2025, EO 14146 revoked most of EO 13961 [20]. On January 20, 2025, Trump gave his inaugural address: "the golden age of America begins right now" [2].
