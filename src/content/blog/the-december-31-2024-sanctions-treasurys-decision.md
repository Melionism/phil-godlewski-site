All right, guys, on the last day of 2024, Treasury used EO 13848 against two organizations it said tried to interfere in the 2024 election. One was Iran's Cognitive Design Production Center, which Treasury called a subsidiary of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps that had planned influence operations "since at least 2023" to inflame socio-political tensions among U.S. voters [1]. The other was the Moscow-based Center for Geopolitical Expertise, which Treasury said was affiliated with Russia's military intelligence agency, the GRU, was founded by previously sanctioned Aleksandr Dugin, used generative AI to produce disinformation, and spread it through a network of at least 100 websites made to look like real news outlets [1]. Treasury also designated the center's director, Valery Korovin [1]. Iran and Russia both denied interfering [2].

Here's the decision, early listings, the Iranian target, the Russian target, and the reaction.

**Quick answer**

- December 2024 election interference sanctions: Treasury action of December 31, 2024 [1].
- Targets: Cognitive Design Production Center (Iran), Center for Geopolitical Expertise (Russia), and director Valery Korovin [1].
- Authorities: EO 13848, plus EO 13694, EO 14024 and CAATSA for the Russian center [1].
- Reaction: both governments denied it [2].

## The Iranian target

Treasury said the center worked for the IRGC and planned operations to inflame tensions among U.S. voters [1]. The Russian side, for comparison: [the Moscow organization named by Treasury](/blog/center-geopolitical-expertise/).

## The Russian target

Treasury said the center [1]:

1. Worked with a GRU unit and got GRU funding.
2. Produced deepfakes about 2024 candidates.
3. Used AI on its own server to avoid Western hosts.
4. Manipulated a video to make baseless accusations against a 2024 vice presidential candidate.
5. Ran 100+ fake news sites.

## Reaction

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- **Treasury**: "The United States will remain vigilant against adversaries who would undermine our democracy" [1].
- **Iran's UN mission**: Iran has repeatedly denied interfering, and its position "remains the same" [2].
- **Russia's embassy**: denied the allegations, saying it respects "the will of the American people" [2].
