All right, guys, here's a term that causes more confusion than any other. What is AUM? It stands for assets under management: the total market value of money a firm invests on behalf of clients. When BlackRock reports $15.5 trillion in AUM at September 30, 2026 [1], that's client money it invests, not money it owns. BlackRock itself is owned by its shareholders, with no single majority shareholder [2].

Here's the definition, managing vs owning, how fees work, and the common misunderstandings.

**Quick answer**

- Assets under management meaning: client money a firm invests [1].
- AUM vs ownership: clients own the assets; the firm earns fees [2].
- How fees work: usually a percentage of the assets.
- BlackRock example: $15.5 trillion AUM, $24.2 billion revenue in 2025 [1][2].

## What is the definition?

AUM meaning, in one line: the value of everything a firm manages for clients. It rises when markets rise or new money comes in, and falls when markets drop or clients leave. See [the size of the firm over time](/blog/blackrock-assets-under-management/).

## Managing vs owning

| Question | Manager | Client |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who owns the assets? | No | Yes |
| Who picks the investments? | Yes, within the mandate | Sets the mandate |
| Who gets the gains? | No (except fees) | Yes |
| Who can take the money out? | No | Yes |

I need you to see that the client keeps ownership. If you own an index fund, you own the shares of the fund. The manager runs it.

## How do fees work?

A manager typically charges a fee as a percentage of assets. That's why AUM matters to the manager: more assets, more fees. BlackRock's 2025 revenue was $24.2 billion on about $14.0 trillion in year-end AUM [2]. Simple math puts revenue at well under 1% of assets.

## What are the common misunderstandings?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **"BlackRock owns $15.5 trillion."** It manages it [1].
2. **"Aladdin controls $25 trillion."** Firms use it to manage that much [3]. See [why platform assets do not equal control](/blog/assets-aladdin-platform/).
3. **"The two numbers add up."** They overlap. See [the gap between $15.5 trillion and $25 trillion](/blog/blackrock-aum-vs-aladdin-assets/).

## FAQ

**What is AUM?** Assets under management: client money a firm invests [1].

**What is the assets under management meaning?** The total value a manager invests for clients.

**What is the AUM meaning for owners?** Clients own the assets; the manager earns fees [2].

**What is AUM vs ownership?** Managing is control within a mandate; owning is the right to the assets.

**Does BlackRock own its AUM?** No; it manages it for clients [1].
