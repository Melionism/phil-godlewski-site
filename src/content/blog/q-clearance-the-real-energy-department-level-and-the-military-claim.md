All right, guys, the "Q" in QAnon comes from a real thing: a security clearance. Here's what it actually is and what it doesn't prove. The U.S. Department of Energy issues two main clearances: L and Q. ClearanceJobs puts it simply: "Where the Defense Department has Top Secret, DoE has Q," and where DoD has Secret, DOE has L [1]. Q clearance gives access to Restricted Data, defined as "all data concerning design, manufacture or utilization of atomic weapons," plus special nuclear material [1]. So it's a nuclear-related clearance at the DOE, not a military rank and not a code for military intelligence. The anonymous poster started out as "Q Clearance Patriot" and claimed that credential [2][3]. Believers read it as a sign Q was a military team. Nothing has confirmed that. A clearance, even if real, wouldn't make any post true; it would only mean access to certain information. And no Q drop has come with proof that its author held any clearance. Linguistic studies point to forum figures, Paul Furber and Ron Watkins, both of whom deny being Q [4].

Here's the real clearance, Dan Scavino as Q, earlier insiders, and what it doesn't prove.

**Quick answer**

- Q clearance meaning: a Department of Energy clearance, equivalent to Top Secret [1].
- Access: Restricted Data on nuclear weapons [1].
- Q's claim: "Q Clearance Patriot," unverified [2][3].
- Doesn't prove: that Q was military or that posts were true.
