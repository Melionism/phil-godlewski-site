All right, guys, if there's one drop people keep coming back to, it's 34. Here's what it said, the dates in it, and why it's still talked about in 2026. Drop #34 went up on November 1, 2017, at 10:56 PM Eastern, signed by the anonymous "Q Clearance Patriot" [1]. It said a series of events was "slated to occur 11.3 upon the arrest announcement of Mr. Podesta (actionable 11.4)" [1]. It warned that "Public riots are being organized in serious numbers," that "a state of temporary military control will be actioned," and that the "Emergency Broadcast System (EMS)" would be used to send messages to citizens [2]. It also said: "Confirmation (to the public) of what is occurring will then be revealed and will not be openly accepted" [2]. Earlier drops set the same dates: drop #15 said "11.3 - Podesta indicted" [1]. What happened: no arrest of John Podesta was announced on November 3 or 4, 2017. His brother Tony stepped down from his lobbying firm on October 30 amid the Mueller probe [3]. The November 4 protests were small and largely peaceful [4]. In September 2026, a White House clip flipping a TV from channel 3 to 4 brought drop 34 back into the conversation [5].

Here's Podesta, military control, explaining the failure, and the dates.

## Podesta

The drop named John Podesta, Hillary Clinton's 2016 campaign chairman. He was never arrested or charged; in 2024 he was named the administration's senior adviser on international climate policy [3][6]. More: [was john podesta arrested](/blog/john-podesta-arrested/).

The drop said "temporary military control will be actioned" [1]. In U.S. law, that would run through the Insurrection Act, which didn't happen. More: [temporary military control q](/blog/temporary-military-control-q/).
