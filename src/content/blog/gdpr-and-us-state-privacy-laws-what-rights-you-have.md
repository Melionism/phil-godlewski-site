All right, guys, what rights do you actually have over your data? Depends where you live. In Europe, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) has applied since May 2018 and gives people strong rights: to access their data, correct it, delete it and object to its use [1]. In the US there's no single federal privacy law. Instead, states are writing their own. California's CCPA gives residents the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of their data [2], and by mid-2026 about two dozen states had comprehensive privacy laws [3].

Here's the GDPR, the US state laws, your rights and what's missing federally.

**Quick answer**

- US state privacy laws: about 24 states have comprehensive laws [3].
- CCPA vs GDPR: both give access and deletion rights; GDPR is broader and covers all processing [1][2].
- Consumer privacy rights: know, access, delete, correct, opt out of sale or targeted ads.
- Federal gap: no single US law covers everyone.

## GDPR

The EU law applies to companies handling data of people in the EU, wherever the company is based [1]. Companies need a legal reason to process data, and big violations can bring big fines. It affects many of the same [companies that trade your personal details](/blog/data-brokers/).

## US state laws

| Correct | Yes [1] | Yes [2] | Usually |
| Opt out of sale | Object to processing [1] | Yes [2] | Yes |

## Your rights

I need you to remember these five:

1. **Know** what's collected.
2. **Access** a copy.
3. **Delete** it, with exceptions.
4. **Correct** errors.
5. **Opt out** of sale and targeted ads.

How you use them depends on your state. Start with the request templates.

## What's missing federally?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **No single national law** for all consumer data.
2. **Patchwork rules:** your rights depend on your state [3].
3. **Gaps:** health apps, data brokers and location data often fall between laws.

Federal agencies like the FTC still act against unfair or deceptive practices, case by case. Use what you have: [the removal process itself](/blog/remove-yourself-from-data-brokers/) and [how to send a data access request](/blog/request-deletion-personal-data/).

## FAQ

**Which US states have privacy laws?** About 24 by mid-2026 [3].

**What's the difference between CCPA and GDPR?** GDPR is broader; both give access and deletion rights [1][2].

**What consumer privacy rights do I have?** Usually know, access, delete, correct and opt out.

**Is there a federal privacy law?** Not a comprehensive one.

**Does GDPR protect Americans?** Only when their data is handled under its scope, such as in the EU.
