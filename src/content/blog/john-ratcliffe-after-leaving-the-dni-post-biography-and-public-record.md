All right, guys, let's look at John Ratcliffe after DNI. He left the Director of National Intelligence post when the term ended in January 2021 [1]. Over the next four years he worked as a senior adviser at American Global Strategies, co-chaired a center at the America First Policy Institute, and joined the Heritage Foundation as a visiting fellow [2][3]. On January 23, 2025, the Senate confirmed him as CIA Director, 74–25 [1].

He's one of the names in the "shadow government" story [4]. Here's his record, year by year.

**Quick answer**

- Before 2021: mayor of Heath, Texas; US attorney; Congressman for Texas's 4th District; DNI [1].
- 2022: senior adviser at American Global Strategies [2].
- 2023: Heritage Foundation visiting fellow [3].
- 2025: CIA Director, the first person to hold both top intelligence posts [1].

## What did he do after January 2021?

He moved into the private sector and think tanks. On July 1, 2022, American Global Strategies named him to a senior advisory role [2]. The firm was founded by Robert O'Brien, the former national security adviser, and Alexander Gray [2]. The same report calls Ratcliffe co-chair of the America First Policy Institute's Center for American Security [2].

In June 2023, the Heritage Foundation announced him as a visiting fellow for national security, cybersecurity and intelligence [3]. He said: "I am excited and grateful for the opportunity to now contribute to those efforts as a visiting fellow" [3].
