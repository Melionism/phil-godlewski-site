All right, guys, let's open the hood. How does BlackRock Aladdin work? In short: it takes in a firm's positions and market data, runs them through risk models, and puts the results, the rule checks and the trading tools on one screen. BlackRock's 10-K says it provides "risk analytics, portfolio management, trade execution, and investment operations" [1]. BlackRock's own page calls it a platform built on "a common data language" [2].

Here's the path from data to decisions, the name, how it grew, one portfolio as an example, and what ties it all to risk.

**Quick answer**

- Input: holdings, prices, market data.
- Engine: risk models and analytics [1].
- Output: risk views, compliance checks, trade tools [1].
- Hosting: on Microsoft Azure since a 2020 partnership [3].

## From data to decisions

Here's the Aladdin portfolio workflow, step by step. For the overview, see [blackrock aladdin](/blog/blackrock-aladdin/).

1. **Data comes in.** Positions, prices, ratings, trades.
2. **It gets one format.** That's the "common data language" [2].
3. **Models run.** Risk measures like value-at-risk, duration and stress scenarios [4].
4. **People act.** Portfolio managers and traders see the results and decide.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Data | Holdings and market data in one format | [2] |
| Analytics | Risk models and scenarios | [1][4] |
| Workflow | Portfolio management, trading, operations | [1] |
| Infrastructure | Cloud hosting on Azure | [3] |

## What does the name stand for?

Asset, Liability, Debt and Derivative Investment Network [5]. Read [what does aladdin stand for](/blog/aladdin-stand-blackrock/).

## How did the system evolve?

From an in-house tool in 1988 to a product sold to other firms [5][1]. In 2020, BlackRock moved Aladdin's infrastructure to Microsoft Azure; its COO said "As both a user and a provider of Aladdin, this decision reflects BlackRock's ongoing commitment to continuous innovation" [3]. See the [history of blackrock aladdin](/blog/history-blackrock-aladdin/).

## What does one portfolio look like on it?

Say a pension fund owns stocks, bonds and some swaps. Aladdin shows what it all is worth, how it would move if rates jump, and whether any rule is about to be broken. The Bank of Israel used it to study Russia–Ukraine risk with stress scenarios [4]. Read [blackrock aladdin explained simply](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-explained-simply/).
