All right, guys, let's go back to the beginning. The history of BlackRock Aladdin starts in 1988. That year, eight people, including Larry Fink and Rob Kapito, founded BlackRock [1], and Aladdin was first launched [2]. The firm started as a boutique fixed income manager [1]. Today Aladdin is the core of a technology business that brought in $2.0 billion in 2025 [3].

When was Aladdin created? 1988. Here's the founders' risk problem, the first system, the opening to outside clients, and where it stands today.

**Quick answer**

- Aladdin 1988: launched the year BlackRock was founded [1][2].
- Founders: eight people, including Larry Fink and Rob Kapito [1].
- Opening up: Aladdin became a product for outside firms; by 2017, 85+ asset-manager clients [4].
- Today: hosted on Azure since 2020, with eFront, Climate, Wealth and Copilot added [5][6][7].

## What was the founders' risk problem?

A bond shop has to know what its bonds, mortgages and derivatives will do when rates move. The founders built their business around that question. The name they gave the system lists it: Asset, Liability, Debt and Derivative Investment Network [2]. For what happens inside, see [what happens inside the system](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-work/).

## What was the first system?

An in-house tool, launched in 1988 [2], to measure risk across BlackRock's own fixed income portfolios. BlackRock went public in October 1999 [1].

## When did it open to outside clients?

The system BlackRock built for itself became something it sells. By 2017, BlackRock said more than 85 asset managers used it, with about $18 trillion on the platform [4]. In the 2008 crisis, the New York Fed retained BlackRock to manage the Maiden Lane portfolios [8]. That put the firm's risk skills on the public stage.

## What is Aladdin today?

I need you to see how far it went. "Aladdin Enterprise is the Company's proprietary technology platform," the 2025 10-K says [3]. It runs on Azure [5]. It added private markets with eFront [6], climate analytics in 2020 [9], and generative AI with Aladdin Copilot [7]. See [Aladdin shown on one sample portfolio](/blog/blackrock-aladdin-explained-simply/) and [the Aladdin acronym explained](/blog/aladdin-stand-blackrock/).
