All right, guys, when people argue about Q, half the fights come from mixing up dates. So here's the timeline in one place. The first posts appeared on October 28, 2017, on 4chan's /pol/ board, in a thread called "The Calm Before the Storm" [1][2]. One of the earliest predicted that Hillary Clinton "will be arrested between 7:45 AM – 8:30 AM EST on Monday" [2]. The poster was first known as "Q Clearance Patriot" [3]. After claiming 4chan had been "infiltrated," Q moved to 8chan in late 2017 and early 2018 [1]. In March 2018 many of Q's tripcodes were cracked, and that August their passwords were published online [4]. 8chan went offline in August 2019 after it lost its service provider; about three months later its owners launched 8kun, and Q resumed posting there [1]. Q's last post before a long silence was on December 8, 2020 [1]. On June 24, 2022, a new post on 8kun talked about playing "the game" again [1]. Researchers count about 4,949 distinct drops in the canonical set [4]. In 2026 the topic came back as people read new government posts through a Q lens.

Here are the key dates, tripcodes, earlier anonymous insiders, why 2026 revived the topic, and how Q relates to bigger events.

**Quick answer**

- Moves: 8chan (2017–2018), 8kun (late 2019) [1].
- Silence: December 8, 2020, to June 24, 2022 [1].
- Size: about 4,949 distinct drops [4].

## Tripcodes

A tripcode is a hash of a password that lets an anonymous poster keep a recognizable signature [4]. Q's codes were cracked in 2018 [4]. More: [what is a tripcode](/blog/tripcode/).

## Earlier anonymous insiders

Q wasn't the first. FBI Anon posted in one session on July 2, 2016, and HLI Anon ran long Q&A sessions from June 2016 to March 2017 [5]. More: [earlier anonymous insiders on 4chan](/blog/fbianon-hlianon/).
