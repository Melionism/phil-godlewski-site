All right, guys, two names come up together a lot: Kash Patel and Devin Nunes. In the community's reading of the shadow government, they're insiders who stayed close to Trump after 2021. On the record: Patel was confirmed as FBI Director on February 20, 2025, by a 51–49 vote [1]. Nunes left Congress to lead Trump Media and Truth Social, and in January 2025 joined the President's Intelligence Advisory Board, which he now chairs [2].

Q pointed to both years earlier. Post 1481, June 12, 2018: "KASHyap Patel - name to remember" [3]. Here are the facts and the interpretations, kept apart.

**Quick answer**

- Patel: former federal prosecutor, NSC counterterrorism director, Pentagon chief of staff, FBI Director since 2025 [1].
- Nunes: Congressman, then Trump Media CEO from late 2021, then Intelligence Advisory Board chairman [2].
- Q mentions: Patel in post 1481; Nunes in at least 12 posts, from August 9, 2018 [3][4].
- Patel's FBI swearing-in: February 21, 2025 [5].

## What is still unproven?

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me." Two things have no public document behind them:

1. **That either man held power from 2021 to 2025.** Nunes ran a company, and the sources here show no federal post for either man from 2021 to 2024 [1][2].
