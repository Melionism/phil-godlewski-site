All right, guys, here's a practical problem with proof-of-citizenship rules that hits a lot of conservative families too. Your birth certificate shows the name you were born with. If you married and took your spouse's name, divorced, or legally changed your name, the name on your proof of citizenship won't match the name on your ID or your voter registration. The University of Maryland survey that estimated 21.3 million citizens lack easy access to citizenship documents didn't even count these mismatches, so its authors called their estimate for women a "lower bound" [1]. Under the House-passed SAVE America Act, a birth record must be paired with a government photo ID, and states must set up a process for applicants who can't provide standard proof to submit other evidence and sign an attestation under penalty of perjury [2]. Address changes matter too: if you move, you re-register, and under the bill that would mean showing documents again, in person [3].

Here's who's affected, name changes, address changes, and what to do.

**Quick answer**

- Name change proof of citizenship voter: a name that differs from your birth certificate needs a linking document.
- Who: people who married, divorced or legally changed names [1].
- Address changes: moving means re-registering, which under the bill means documents in person [3].
- Fix now: keep certified copies of marriage certificates, divorce decrees or court orders.

## Address changes

Moving within a state may only need an update. Moving to a new state means a new registration. Under the SAVE America Act, new registrants would show proof of citizenship, which election officials say effectively ends registering by mail or online [3].

## What to do

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **Find your birth certificate or passport.**
2. **Check every name change** and its document.
3. **Order certified copies** from the county or state where the event happened.
4. **Consider a passport**, which shows your current name and citizenship in one document.

## FAQ

**Does a name change affect proof of citizenship?** It can, if your documents don't match.

**What links the names?** A marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order.

**Does moving matter?** Yes, if you register in a new state [3].

**Is this required now?** Not federally; some states have their own rules.

**What's the simplest fix?** A current passport.
