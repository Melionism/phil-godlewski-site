All right, guys, a lot of people heard the July 16 address and came away saying, "Trump ordered prosecutions." Let's look at exactly what he asked for. In the speech, he said he was "asking the FBI director to ensure that the matter is fully investigated" and to "work with the Department of Justice to prosecute those responsible for any crimes" [1]. The White House release says the president directed the Director of National Intelligence, the Justice Department, the FBI and the CIA "to investigate how the intelligence was hidden from the sitting President and the public," and to hold those responsible accountable, "including potential criminal charges" [2]. That's a direction to investigate. Whether anyone gets charged still depends on evidence, prosecutors, a grand jury and, often, the calendar.

Here's the Obama question, criminal referrals, presidential direction and following the cases.

**Quick answer**

- Trump DOJ directive 2020 election: investigate how intelligence was "hidden" and prosecute "any crimes" [1][2].
- Trump orders DOJ election prosecutions: he asked for investigation and prosecution if crimes are found; no names were charged in the speech [1].
- DOJ 2020 election investigation: the FBI also searched Fulton County's election office in January 2026 [3].
- Legal weight: a direction to investigate isn't an indictment.
