All right, guys, everybody talks about the intelligence report under EO 13848. Fewer people know there's a second report. Under Section 1(b), within 45 days of receiving the DNI's assessment, the Attorney General and the Secretary of Homeland Security must report to the President and senior officials on whether foreign interference "materially affected the security or integrity" of election infrastructure, or of the infrastructure of political organizations, campaigns or candidates [1]. For 2020, that report was classified, but DOJ and DHS released a joint public summary on March 16, 2021. It said claims that foreign governments owned or controlled election infrastructure or altered vote tallies "were not credible," and that the departments "found no evidence that any foreign government-affiliated actor manipulated election results" [2]. It also said Russian, Chinese and Iranian government-affiliated actors did compromise the security of some networks [2].

Here's what they review, the deadline, what's published and what it isn't.

**Quick answer**

- EO 13848 attorney general DHS report: a second review under Section 1(b) [1].
- Focus: election and campaign infrastructure, not voter persuasion [1][2].
- Deadline: 45 days after receiving the DNI assessment [1].
- 2020 result: no evidence any foreign government-affiliated actor manipulated results [2].

## What they review

| Question | Covered? |
| --- | --- |
| Was election infrastructure affected? | Yes [1] |
| Was campaign or candidate infrastructure affected? | Yes [1] |
| Unresolved factual disputes | Must be listed [1] |
| Remedial steps other than sanctions | May be recommended [1] |
| Propaganda aimed at voters | No; that's the DNI's broader assessment [2] |

The order's wording: [the text itself](/blog/eo-13848-full-text/).

## Deadline

1. **Election day.**
2. **+45 days**: DNI assessment [1].
3. **+45 days after receipt**: AG and DHS report [1].

For 2020, the DNI assessment was dated March 10, 2021, and the DOJ-DHS summary came March 16, 2021, after the deadlines in the order had passed [2][3].

## What's published

The reports go to the President and senior officials. Nothing in the order requires publication [1]. For 2020, the public got the DNI's declassified assessment and the DOJ-DHS joint summary [2][3].

## What it isn't

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

- **It isn't a sanctions decision.** Treasury decides those: [Treasury's rule implementing the order](/blog/31-cfr-part-579/).
- **It isn't a review of domestic fraud.** It covers foreign actors [1].
- **It isn't a court finding.**
