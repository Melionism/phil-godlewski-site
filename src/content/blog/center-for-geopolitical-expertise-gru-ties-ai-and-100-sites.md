All right, guys, the second target of Treasury's December 31, 2024 action is the one people should study, because it shows where foreign meddling is going. Treasury described the Center for Geopolitical Expertise, or CGE, as a Moscow-based organization affiliated with Russia's military intelligence, the GRU, founded by Aleksandr Dugin, who was already under U.S. sanctions [1]. According to Treasury, CGE worked with a GRU unit, got GRU funding, and directed and funded deepfakes and disinformation about 2024 candidates [1]. It used generative AI tools on its own server, which Treasury said let it avoid Western web hosts, and spread the content through at least 100 websites designed to look like legitimate news outlets, so the stories seemed to back each other up [1]. Treasury said CGE also manipulated a video to make baseless accusations against a 2024 vice presidential candidate [1]. Its director, Valery Korovin, was sanctioned too [1]. Russia's embassy denied the allegations [2].

Here's the entity, GRU ties, AI and sites, and what was said.

**Quick answer**

- Center for Geopolitical Expertise: a GRU-affiliated Moscow organization founded by Aleksandr Dugin, per Treasury [1].
- Sanctioned: December 31, 2024, under EO 13848, EO 13694, EO 14024 and CAATSA section 224 [1].
- Methods: AI-generated content, deepfakes and 100+ fake news sites [1].
- Russia's response: denial [2].
