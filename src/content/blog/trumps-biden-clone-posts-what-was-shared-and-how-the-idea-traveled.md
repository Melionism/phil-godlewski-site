It added: "#Democrats dont know the difference."

That's the Trump Truth Social Biden clone moment. It ran during a stretch of late-night posts from about 9:40 p.m. to 12:37 a.m. Here's what was posted, where the idea came from and how it spread.

- What: a reshare of a post about Biden clones and robots.
- From whom: a small account; Trump added no comment of his own.

## How does amplification work?

Here's the path, step by step:

1. **A small account posts.** Few followers, few eyes.
2. **A big account reshares.** Now millions see it.
3. **News covers the reshare.** Now people who never follow either account see it.
4. **Screenshots travel.** The claim lives on without its source.

That's how a Trump Biden double post becomes a national story in a weekend. The claim didn't change. The audience did.
