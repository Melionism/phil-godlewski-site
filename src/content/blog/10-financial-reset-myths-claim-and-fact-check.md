All right, guys, here are ten financial reset myths I see every week, checked against official records. Short version: some start from real facts, like the stablecoin law signed July 18, 2025 [1] or the executive order against a US central bank digital currency [2]. Then they get stretched into one big switch that no law, treaty or central bank has announced.

Here are myths about dates, money and gold, accounts, and how to check the next claim.

**Quick answer**

- Financial reset debunked: no law or treaty sets a global reset date.
- Great reset myths: a 2020 conference phrase stretched into a plan.
- Financial collapse myths: crises happen, but modern ones ended in rescues [3].
- Check: law, date, document.

## Myths about dates

| # | Claim | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## How to check the next claim

This isn't a "trust me" thing. This is "verify me."

1. **Which law?** Find the bill number.
2. **Which date?** Find it in the law, not a video.
3. **Which agency?** Find its own announcement.
4. **What's true in it?** Many myths start from a real fact.

See [how reform differs from a crisis or collapse](/blog/financial-system-restructuring/).
