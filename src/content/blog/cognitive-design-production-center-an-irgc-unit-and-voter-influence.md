All right, guys, here's one of the two organizations Treasury hit at the end of 2024. The Cognitive Design Production Center, or CDPC, is described by Treasury as a subsidiary of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps that, "since at least 2023," planned influence operations meant to inflame socio-political tensions among U.S. voters ahead of the 2024 election [1]. Treasury designated it under EO 13848 on December 31, 2024 [1]. Iran's UN mission said Iran has repeatedly denied interfering in U.S. elections [2]. It's part of a pattern: Treasury had already sanctioned the IRGC and its media fronts in October 2020, an Iranian cyber firm over threatening emails in 2020, and an IRGC-linked hacker over a 2024 campaign leak [3][4][5].

Here's the entity, Treasury's allegations, methods and context.

- Sanctioned: December 31, 2024, under EO 13848 [1].
- Allegation: planning influence operations to inflame U.S. social tensions [1].
- Iran's response: denial [2].
